summary: 2016年10月19日，在《宝可梦 太阳·月亮》发售前不足一个月之际，GamesRadar+ 编辑 Connor Sheridan 通过口译专访了 GAME FREAK 总监大森滋。大森滋在采访中回应了三个核心问题：其一，Z招式并非一击必胜的强力大招，而是经过大量平衡性调整且每场对战限用一次，所有宝可梦均可使用，有望让以往冷门的宝可梦在对战中焕发新用法；其二，《宝可梦 太阳》与《宝可梦 月亮》设定了 12 小时时差，使玩家在同一现实时间分别遇见猫鼬少与小拉达（阿罗拉的样子）等昼夜相异的宝可梦，借此鼓励玩家讨论、交换与对战；其三，本作的根本主题与标题灵感皆源于“生命”——太阳维系生命，月亮则牵动引力与生物节律。

- type: heading
  level: 2
  original: "Is there more to the Z-Moves than being super powerful?"
  translation: "Z招式除了威力惊人之外，还有更深的讲究吗？"
- role: answer
  speaker: 大森滋
  original: "\"With the Z-Moves we've had our staff take a lot of time considering the battle system and making sure that it really is balanced, adjusting the power of the moves, the effects of the moves, and everything like that. So it certainly isn't the case that just by using these Z-Moves you're going to absolutely win the battle and everything's going to be very easy. There's also the restriction that you can only use a Z-Move once per battle. So this idea of deciding when is the best time to activate this Z-Move and use it is actually going to, we think, add a really deep layer to the battles and the strategy behind it."
  translation: "“关于Z招式，我们让开发人员花了很多时间去考量对战系统，确保整体足够平衡，包括调整招式的威力、效果以及方方面面的细节。因此，绝不是只要使出Z招式就能稳赢、让对战变得轻而易举。此外，每场对战只能使用一次Z招式。我们认为，判断究竟在哪个时机发动Z招式最为合适，将会为对战及其背后的策略性增添非常深厚的层次。"
- role: answer
  speaker: 大森滋
  original: "\"The Z-Moves themselves can be used by any Pokemon at all. So hopefully this will give rise to people using even more Pokemon than before, a wider range than we've seen previously. Perhaps some Pokemon that weren't particularly popular in battles up until now, people will find new ways to use these Pokemon and really make a big difference in battle depending on which Z-Moves they want to use with these Pokemon.\""
  translation: "“Z招式本身是任何宝可梦都可以使出的。因此我们希望，这能让玩家在对战中起用比以往更多、种类更广泛的宝可梦。也许某些过去在对战中不太热门的宝可梦，会因为玩家发掘出搭配不同Z招式的新用法，而在对战中发挥出截然不同的关键作用。”"
- type: heading
  level: 2
  original: "Why do Pokemon Sun and Moon take place at opposite times of day?"
  translation: "为什么《宝可梦 太阳》与《宝可梦 月亮》的昼夜时间正好相反？"
- role: answer
  speaker: 大森滋
  original: "\"One big change in this idea of time we've got for Sun and Moon is that Sun and Moon are set 12 hours apart. So in Pokemon Sun, you'll see the time will reflect the actual time outside, the standard time it is where you are. Whereas Pokemon Moon is set 12 hours further ahead, so it'll be nighttime when it's day where you're playing. This lets you find different Pokemon at the same time of day. So for example a player playing Pokemon Sun might wander around and find a Yungoos during the day. But at the same time a player playing Pokemon Moon, though it's daytime in the real world, they're gonna find nighttime Pokemon in the game. So they might encounter an Alola Rattata for example."
  translation: "“我们在《太阳·月亮》的时间概念上做了一项很大的改动，那就是《太阳》与《月亮》之间相差了 12 个小时。在《宝可梦 太阳》中，游戏时间与你所处现实世界的标准时间同步；而《宝可梦 月亮》则快了 12 个小时，所以当现实里是白天时，游戏里会是夜晚。这样一来，在一天中的同一时刻，两个版本也能遇见不同的宝可梦。举例来说，玩《宝可梦 太阳》的玩家在白天四处探索时可能会遇到猫鼬少；但在同一时间，玩《宝可梦 月亮》的玩家尽管现实中也是白天，在游戏里遇到的却是夜间出没的宝可梦，比如小拉达（阿罗拉的样子）。"
- role: answer
  speaker: 大森滋
  original: "\"With all of these different things we're hoping to really encourage players to communicate with one another. They're going to see these different experiences and talk about them, trade, perhaps battle through these different but also shared experiences that they can really enjoy and get together over.\""
  translation: "“通过这些差异化的设计，我们非常希望能鼓励玩家们彼此交流。大家会经历各自不同的体验，并围绕这些既各异又共通的经历展开讨论、互相交换，甚至切磋对战，真正聚在一起享受游戏的乐趣。”"
- type: heading
  level: 2
  original: "What's the game about, really?"
  translation: "这部作品真正想表达的主题是什么？"
- role: answer
  speaker: 大森滋
  original: "\"The basic idea is that of life. One thing we thought of is the sun, obviously vital for life. And the moon affects gravity and the rhythms of living things. All of these different aspects inspired the way we chose to go about this game and the theme, and the titles of the game itself.\""
  translation: "“最核心的理念是‘生命’。我们首先想到的是太阳——它对生命而言显然不可或缺；而月亮则影响着引力与生物的节律。正是这些不同层面的联想，启发了我们构思这款游戏的方向、主题，乃至于游戏本身的标题。”"
