- original: We're big Froakie fans too! Sorry Chespin, no love for you here today. So, why did you decide to name this generation ‘X’ and ‘Y’? Do they hold any significance beyond the design of legendary Pokémon Xerneas and Yvetal?
  role: question
  speaker: Nintendo Life
  translation: 我们也是呱呱泡蛙的大粉丝！抱歉了哈力栗，今天这里没人爱你。那么，你们为什么决定把这一世代命名为“X”和“Y”？除了传说宝可梦哲尔尼亚斯和伊裴尔塔尔的设计之外，它们还有其他含义吗？
- original: We actually came up with the titles before we even had the legendary Pokémon designs. The ‘X’ and ‘Y’ titles come from the X and Y axis and we used these as ways to represent different ways of thought, different opinions, but also where these axis overlap. The message that we wanted to send and the theme we had in mind was finding that common ground that we all share.
  role: answer
  speaker: 增田顺一
  translation: 其实我们连传说宝可梦的设计都还没有的时候，就已经想好了标题。“X”和“Y”这两个标题来自X轴和Y轴，我们用它们来代表不同的思维方式、不同的观点，同时也代表这些轴相交的地方。我们想传达的信息和心中的主题，就是找到我们共有的共同点。
- original: When it came to the legendary Pokémon design we knew that when viewed from far away we wanted them to look kind of like an X or a Y, so that was the main direction we went for.
  role: answer
  speaker: 吉田宏信
  translation: 说到传说宝可梦的设计，我们知道从远处看时，希望它们看起来有点像X或Y，所以这就是我们主要的方向。
- original: In Pokémon Black and White 2 there was a key system, which helped you catch the Regis and gave you access to version exclusive content, such as the Black City and the White Forest. Will there be a similar feature in X and Y?
  role: question
  speaker: Nintendo Life
  translation: 在《宝可梦 黑2·白2》中有一个钥匙系统，它可以帮助你捕捉雷吉系列宝可梦，并让你访问版本限定内容，比如黑色城市和白色森林。X和Y中会有类似的功能吗？
- original: In regards to the key system that existed in Black and White 2 that was something that we implemented after we had the balance right in Pokémon Black and White, so we put that in to add in some more options as a sequel to the game. But as X and Y is a totally new entry we have added a lot of new changes, and completely rebalanced the game, so we didn’t really think about putting in a similar system this time around.
  role: answer
  speaker: 增田顺一
  translation: 关于《黑2／白2》中存在的钥匙系统，那是我们在《宝可梦 黑·白》的平衡调整好之后才实装的，所以作为游戏的续作，我们加入它是为了增加一些选项。但X和Y是全新的作品，我们加入了许多新变化，并完全重新平衡了游戏，所以这次我们并没有真正考虑加入类似的系统。

- original: The theme of X and Y is 'beauty' and France matches that. 'Kalos' is actually an ancient Greek word that means 'beauty'.
  role: answer
  speaker: 增田顺一

- original: What has it been like moving into fully 3D environments when you've spent so long making the core series in a top-down viewpoint?
  role: question
  speaker: Nintendo Life
  translation: 在花了这么长时间以俯视视角制作核心系列之后，转向全3D环境感觉如何？
- original: This time with the move to full 3D you can freely manipulate the camera angles, which you couldn’t do before. Being able to move cameras around freely and frame the scene just how we wanted added a lot of new possibilities, but at the same time a few challenges. It was difficult trying to get that right. For example, we can show scenes from any angle, but due to hardware limitations we can’t show things that are really far away, the rendering power isn’t quite there so there’s a drop in performance. Because of this we had to find ways around this, for example if we wanted to show a town from far away we can do that camera angle but we have to maybe use a lower polygon model for the background. So there’s a lot of new challenges that we have to deal with by having this new freedom with the camera angles.
  role: answer
  speaker: 增田顺一
  translation: 这次转向全3D后，你可以自由操控镜头角度，这是以前做不到的。能够自由移动镜头并按照我们想要的方式构图，带来了许多新的可能性，但同时也带来了一些挑战。要把它做好很困难。例如，我们可以从任何角度展示场景，但由于硬件限制，我们无法展示非常遥远的东西，渲染能力还不够，所以性能会下降。因此我们必须想办法绕过这个问题，比如如果我们想从远处展示一个城镇，我们可以采用那个镜头角度，但也许必须为背景使用多边形数量较低的模型。所以，由于镜头角度有了这种新的自由，我们必须应对许多新的挑战。

I’ve always wanted to be able to reach out and pet Pokémon, and with Pokémon Amie I’ve finally been able to make that possible, as you can can also play with them.
