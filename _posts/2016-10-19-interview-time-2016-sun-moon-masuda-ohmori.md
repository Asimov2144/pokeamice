  original: "TIME: Did Pokémon Go’s meteoric success alter the development of the game or the pressure you felt given the resurgence of interest in the Pokémon franchise and presumptively higher powered microscope that maybe puts Pokémon Sun and Moon under?"
  translation: 《Pokémon GO》的迅速走红是否改变了本作的开发方向？随着外界对宝可梦系列的关注度再度高涨，《宝可梦 太阳·月亮》难免会被置于更严苛的放大镜下审视，这有没有给你们带来更大的压力？
- role: answer
  speaker: 增田顺一
  original: "Masuda: We began Pokémon Go’s development as a project that would be different from the main series roleplaying games we create at Game Freak. Pokémon Go uses location-based information where you walk around in the real world to find Pokémon and catch Pokémon. Our goal with Pokémon Go was to put the focus on the catching Pokémon element. We wanted people to just enjoy going out and catching Pokémon."
  translation: 我们在启动《Pokémon GO》开发之初，就把它定位为一个有别于 GAME FREAK 所制作的主系列角色扮演游戏的项目。《Pokémon GO》利用位置信息，让玩家在现实世界中四处走动，寻找并捕捉宝可梦。我们对《Pokémon GO》的目标，是把重心放在“捕捉宝可梦”这一要素上，希望大家纯粹享受走出门去捕捉宝可梦的乐趣。
- role: answer
  speaker: 增田顺一
  original: "With the main series games, we instead focus on catching Pokémon but also on raising them and adventuring with them and having a lot of strategically deep battles, where players can then use the Pokémon they caught in those battles. It’s the same with Pokémon Sun and Moon. So Pokémon Go didn’t really affect any of the development decisions, though I do think that thanks to Pokémon Go there’s an increased awareness of Pokémon in general. That’s great for us, and I hope people will be interested in giving Pokémon Sun and Moon a go if they’re looking for a more in-depth experience."
  translation: 而在主系列作品中，我们不仅注重捕捉宝可梦，还注重培育它们、与它们一同冒险，以及展开极具策略深度的对战——玩家可以把自己捕捉到的宝可梦派上对战舞台。《宝可梦 太阳·月亮》也是如此。因此，《Pokémon GO》实际上并没有影响本作的任何开发决策。不过我确实觉得，多亏了《Pokémon GO》，宝可梦整体的知名度得到了提升。这对我们来说是件好事，如果大家想要体验更深入的玩法，我也希望他们会有兴趣来试试《宝可梦 太阳·月亮》。
- role: question
  original: "Mr. Masuda, you’re the producer again this time. Do you enjoy the higher-level view after directing so many of the earlier games?"
  translation: 增田先生，这一次您再次担任制作人。在亲自执导了这么多部早期作品之后，您享受现在这种更偏向宏观全局的视角吗？
- role: answer
  speaker: 增田顺一
  original: "Masuda: Recently I really have come to feel that both roles, producer and director, are a lot of fun in their own rights. When I was directing the games, I was directly involved in the creative aspects and really in the trenches with everyone else. That’s a lot of fun as someone who likes to create things. When you work as a producer, you have more influence over the big picture, and the Pokémon brand as a whole. Making decisions about when to release for example the virtual console versions, or the Red and Green or Red and Blue original Pokémon games and how those would interact with the brand as a whole."
  translation: 最近我确实深切感受到，制作人和总监这两个职位各有各的乐趣。担任总监的时候，我会直接投身创作一线，和大家并肩奋战。对于喜欢亲手创造事物的人来说，这非常有意思。而担任制作人时，你能对整体大局以及整个宝可梦品牌施加更广的影响——例如决定何时推出虚拟主机（Virtual Console）版，或是初代《宝可梦 红·绿》（海外版为《红·蓝》），以及这些作品该如何与整个品牌产生联动。
- role: answer
  speaker: 增田顺一
  original: "I think both roles are a lot of fun, and they both offer unique ways to make your mark on Pokémon. At the same time I’m a producer now. I wouldn’t want to just give up creating things. I think right now I’m able to scratch that itch by working on some of the music in the games."
  translation: 我觉得两个角色都很有意思，也各自提供了为《宝可梦》留下个人印记的独特方式。不过，即便现在身为制作人，我也不想就此放弃亲手创作。我想，如今能参与游戏里一部分音乐的创作，正好解了我的手痒。
- role: question
  original: "You’re anticipating my next question! As the game’s composer, what styles and motifs were you going for this time?"
  translation: 您抢先猜到我的下一个问题了！作为本作的作曲之一，这一次您在音乐风格和主题动机上追求的是怎样的方向？
- role: answer
  speaker: 增田顺一
  original: "Masuda: We took inspiration from Hawaii specifically, but we wanted to do more than just copy the sort of music you’d hear there. The Alola region in Pokémon Sun and Moon is definitely its own thing, but also obviously inspired by traditional Hawaiian music styles."
  translation: 我们的确专门从夏威夷汲取了灵感，但并不想只是照搬当地听到的那种音乐。《宝可梦 太阳·月亮》里的阿罗拉地区归根结底有着属于自己的独特风貌，但显然也受到了夏威夷传统音乐风格的启发。
- role: answer
  speaker: 增田顺一
  original: "So for example, we wanted to use the core rhythms that you would find in a lot of traditional Hawaiian music. But in order to make it unique, we went with completely different melodies over the top of those. In general, I think you’ll find that it invokes feelings of a tropical island environment, so a lot of really warm tones."
  translation: 举个例子，我们希望借用许多夏威夷传统音乐中常见的核心节奏，但为了做出独特性，我们在这些节奏之上谱写了截然不同的旋律。总体而言，我想大家会感受到它所唤起的热带岛屿氛围，因此配乐中运用了大量非常温暖的音色。
- role: question
  original: "Mr. Ohmori, your first directorial outing was with Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire, which were remakes. What was it like shifting from a directorial vantage that was partly looking backward, to creating completely new installments?"
  translation: 大森先生，您首次担任总监的作品是作为重制版的《宝可梦 欧米伽红宝石·阿尔法蓝宝石》。从某种程度上带有回溯性质的执导立场，转向打造一部完全新作，这是一种怎样的体验？
- role: answer
  speaker: 大森滋
  original: "Ohmori: Right after Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire, we wrapped up development and I immediately began working as the director on Pokémon Sun and Moon with no break. I quickly realized that being the director of a remake of a game that had come out over a decade ago, and working on a completely new one, was obviously very different. With Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire, I had a really strong base from which to work, but with Sun and Moon, we of course had to create everything from scratch. So things like the sheer amount of elements that you need to consider in creating a brand new setting, and the game in general, since it’s on a completely different scale from Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire."
  translation: 《宝可梦 欧米伽红宝石·阿尔法蓝宝石》的开发刚一收尾，我便马不停蹄地立刻投入到《宝可梦 太阳·月亮》的总监工作中，中间几乎没有休息。我很快就切身体会到，执导一部十多年前旧作的重制版，与打造一部全新作品，显然是截然不同的两回事。制作《欧米伽红宝石·阿尔法蓝宝石》时，我已经有一套非常扎实的基底可以依托；但到了《太阳·月亮》，我们自然得把一切从零搭建起来。无论是构筑全新舞台时所需权衡的庞大要素，还是整部作品的体量，都和《欧米伽红宝石·阿尔法蓝宝石》完全不在一个层级上。
- role: answer
  speaker: 大森滋
  original: "One of the things that I think really helped me when I was first beginning Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire’s development, was that I went and talked with Mr. Masuda, because he was the director on the original Ruby and Sapphire games. I learned his approach and way of thinking when he first came up with the Hoenn region, the setting of the Ruby and Sapphire game. I used that to change some things in Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire, but I was also able to take those lessons and directly apply them to creating Sun and Moon from scratch."
  translation: 回想当初刚开始开发《欧米伽红宝石·阿尔法蓝宝石》时，有一件事让我获益匪浅——那就是去找增田先生交流，毕竟他正是原版《宝可梦 红宝石·蓝宝石》的总监。我从他那里学到了他当年构思《红宝石·蓝宝石》舞台“丰缘地区”时的切入点与思考方式。我不仅把这些收获用在了《欧米伽红宝石·阿尔法蓝宝石》的各项调整上，还得以直接将这份经验运用到从零打造《太阳·月亮》的过程之中。
- role: answer
  speaker: 大森滋
  original: "Another thing, with Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire being remakes, is that it was more difficult to make some of the more radical changes I was interested in. So one of those examples is trials. We don’t have gyms in the game this time. It’s the first time a Pokémon game’s done that. Instead we have what we call “trials” as a way for players to work through the game and progress. I knew I wanted to put in something, this being a 20th anniversary of the franchise game, that would make the games feel fresh both for the veterans of the series and new players alike."
  translation: 另一点在于，《欧米伽红宝石·阿尔法蓝宝石》毕竟是重制作品，我心里想做的一些更大胆的改动很难施展开来。这方面的一个例子就是“考验”。这一次的游戏里没有道馆，这在《宝可梦》系列历史上还是头一遭。取而代之的，是我们称之为“考验”的玩法，以此推动玩家的游玩体验与剧情进度。既然本作是系列 20 周年纪念作品，我很明确自己想加入一些新东西，让一路追随过来的老玩家和初次接触的新玩家都能耳目一新。
- role: question
  original: "The Pokémon games are viewed as, I don’t want to say simplistic, but definitely a bit easier because they’ve traditionally been aimed at younger players. Assuming the franchise’s audience has expanded demographically, as players who grew up with those earlier games are now in their twenties and thirties, have you done anything with Sun and Moon that caters at all to players looking for more challenging gameplay?"
  translation: 外界普遍认为《宝可梦》系列——我不想说它浅白，但确实偏容易一些，因为它历来主要面向年轻玩家群体。考虑到如今系列的受众年龄层已经不断拓宽，伴随着早期作品长大的玩家现在都二三十岁了，你们在《太阳·月亮》中有没有为追求更高挑战性的玩家做些什么设计？
- role: answer
  speaker: 大森滋
  original: "Ohmori: First and foremost, as you pointed out, with the Pokémon games we always want to focus on newcomers. So making sure that the games are always easy to pick up and play, and that it’s easy for people to understand what to do. At the start, we teach players how to throw Pokeballs and catch Pokémon. That’s something we never want to change, because if we simply made it more difficult for veterans, we’d simultaneously make it less approachable for newcomers."
  translation: 首先，正如您指出的那样，在《宝可梦》系列里，我们始终希望能优先照顾新玩家，确保游戏一直容易上手、让玩家轻松明白该做什么。游戏一开篇，我们就会教玩家如何投掷精灵球、捕捉宝可梦。这一点是我们绝不想改变的，因为如果只是一味为了老玩家提高难度，新玩家同时也会被拒之门外。
- role: answer
  speaker: 大森滋
  original: "That said, for all of the people who’ve been fans of the games over the years and continue to play it, we’ve kept them in mind. Rather than merely raise the difficulty or add other complexities, we’ve tried to keep things fresh by adding new game systems or changing the game systems and certain gameplay elements. Like changing the battle system, for example. A couple of the things we did this time around were, from the battle perspective we introduced these things called Z moves, which are super powerful moves you can use only once in battle. That’s going to dramatically change the battle environment for those players. Also, we have in the Alola region these specific regional variances of existing Pokémon. A lot of players from the original games will see their old favorites, but with new takes."
  translation: 话虽如此，对于多年来一直喜爱并持续游玩本系列的粉丝，我们也一直记挂在心。比起单纯拉高难度或增添繁复的门槛，我们更倾向于通过引入新系统、或是革新既有机制与部分玩法要素来保持新鲜感——比如说调整对战系统。就对战层面而言，这次我们做了几项尝试：一是引入了名为“Z招式”的新要素，那是每场对战只能发动一次的强力招式，这必将为老玩家们大幅改写对战环境；此外，在阿罗拉地区，我们还为既有的宝可梦设计了当地特有的“阿罗拉的样子”，许多从初代一路走来的玩家都能看到昔日喜爱的宝可梦以全新面貌回归。
- role: question
  original: "Hawaii seems in hindsight like a no-brainer as a conceptual inspiration for the setting because of all its unique, proximal biomes. Are there other ways beyond the music or the biomes that the game references Hawaiian culture?"
  translation: 事后回看，夏威夷拥有众多彼此毗邻又各具特色的生态群落，作为地区设定的概念灵感简直顺理成章。除了音乐和生态地貌之外，游戏中还有哪些地方融入了对夏威夷文化的致敬？
- role: answer
  speaker: 大森滋
  original: "Ohmori: I can say personally that I wanted to go with Hawaii as the inspiration for the region the whole time. I go to Hawaii in my personal time, and I really like it. I feel that it’s a place that’s so full of life and really close to nature as a region. Also, just like you said, we wanted to have these really cool, unique biomes. With Hawaii being a region split up into these islands that have all these unique biomes, it really made sense. We wanted to express concepts like the Alola regional variance. When you go to look at the Galapagos Islands, for instance, you see these unique animals that don’t exist elsewhere. That was one of the things we wanted to express, and Hawaii made sense in that respect."
  translation: 就我个人而言，其实我从头到尾都想以夏威夷作为新地区的灵感蓝本。我平时休假就会去夏威夷，非常喜欢那里。我觉得那是一个生机勃勃、与大自然格外亲近的地方。而且正如您所说，我们很想呈现这些极具魅力又各不相同的生态群落。夏威夷本身就由多座岛屿组成，每座岛都拥有独特的自然环境，放在游戏里再合适不过。我们也很想表现“阿罗拉的样子”这样的概念——好比提到加拉帕戈斯群岛，人们就会看到其他地方绝无仅有的奇特生物。这正是我们想传达的理念，从这一角度看，选择夏威夷也合情合理。
- role: answer
  speaker: 大森滋
  original: "Also when we were coming up with names for the games, the lighting you see in Hawaii, the warm sunlight and how the moon can light up the ocean at nighttime, it’s something you really don’t see elsewhere. Or at least I haven’t seen elsewhere. I knew immediately that I wanted to use those kinds of concepts, and to convey them in the names for the game."
  translation: 另外，在构思游戏标题的时候，我在夏威夷见到的光线也给了我很大触动——温暖的阳光，以及夜晚月光照亮整片海面的景象，那是别处很难见到的风景，至少我在其他地方从未见过。我当下就决定要把这样的意象运用起来，并借由游戏的标题传达出去。
- role: question
  original: "Most of the prior games have these materialistic titles, rubies and sapphires or diamonds and pearls. With Sun and Moon, to me the titles suggest you’re reaching for something less tangible. Can you talk a little bit about that switch to celestial objects, and if it’s indicative of a tonal shift for the series?"
  translation: 以往绝大多数作品都采用了偏具象、偏物质化的标题，比如红宝石与蓝宝石，或是钻石与珍珠。而在我看来，《太阳·月亮》这个标题似乎指向了某种更为抽象无形的存在。您能谈谈转向天体意象背后的想法吗？这是否也意味着系列整体基调的转变？
- role: answer
  speaker: 大森滋
  original: "Ohmori: Quite a lot of thought went into the titles of Pokémon Sun and Moon, actually. I think when we first set out on this project, we knew it was going to be released on the 20th anniversary of Pokémon. I knew as the director, I really wanted to focus on Pokémon as these living creatures, so really focusing on them being alive. I kind of wanted this project to be a celebration of that life, and to really express this respect for life. When thinking of life in general, how does life come about on our planet, for example.
