  original: Anyone who played Pokémon in the Game Boy era will remember trading their virtual creatures with other people. It was a convoluted but memorable process.
  translation: 凡是在 Game Boy 时代玩过《宝可梦》的人，都会记得与其他玩家交换宝可梦的经历——那是一套颇为繁琐、却又令人难忘的过程。
- role: narration
  original: I used to take my battered yellow Game Boy round to my best friend's house on a Saturday. He'd bought Pokémon Red, I had Pokémon Blue. We'd sit cross-legged on the floor, eyes fixed on the tiny screens in front us, handhelds connected via a Link-Up cable we'd bought pooling our pocket money.
  translation: 过去周六我常带着那台磕碰磨损的黄色 Game Boy 去我最好的朋友家。他买了《宝可梦 红》，我买了《宝可梦 蓝》。我们会盘腿坐在地板上，紧盯着眼前小小的屏幕，两台掌机之间靠我们凑零花钱买来的连接线连在一起。
- role: narration
  original: But by the time it came to Diamond and Pearl the need for physical cables - or to even be in the same room - was gone. After all, DS had Wi-Fi capability built in.
  translation: 但到了《钻石·珍珠》问世之时，玩家们不再需要实体连接线——甚至不再需要待在同一个房间里了。毕竟任天堂 DS 本身就内置了 Wi-Fi 功能。
- role: narration
  original: Creating a pair of new Pokémon games for Nintendo's dual-screened handheld, Masuda - now producer and director of the Pokémon series, back then a programmer and composer - knew there was an expectation the series would introduce wireless play. It did. Diamond and Pearl included local wireless support, no cables required, but also allowed you to trade Pokémon over the internet via the now-retired Nintendo Wi-Fi Connection service.
  translation: 在为任天堂这台双屏掌机打造这对全新《宝可梦》作品时，如今身兼系列制作人与总监、当年还是程序员兼作曲家的增田顺一深知玩家对系列引入无线联机的期待。他们也确实做到了：《钻石·珍珠》不仅支持无需线缆的本地无线通信，还能通过如今已停运的 Nintendo Wi-Fi Connection 服务，让玩家在互联网上互相交换宝可梦。
- role: narration
  original: Suddenly, players from all over the world could trade and share creatures. It was a neat feature for casual players, but a huge boon for hardcore fans - because while many players were content with filling up their Pokédex, others went far further.
  translation: 一时间，世界各地的玩家都能互相交换并分享各自的宝可梦。对休闲玩家而言这是个贴心有趣的新功能，而对核心玩家来说更是一份天大的馈赠——因为在许多玩家满足于填满宝可梦图鉴的同时，还有一部分人走得更远。
- role: narration
  original: Without getting too technical (or asking about the logistics of Pokémon making babies), Pokémon breeding had fascinated players since its introduction. One feature in particular - the ability to get ultra-rare Shiny Pokémon offspring - intrigued hardcore players.
  translation: 暂且不深究太技术的细节（也不去多问宝可梦培育屋里生蛋的运作原理），自打“生蛋培育”系统加入系列以来，它就深深吸引着玩家们。其中有一项要素尤其令核心玩家着迷：孵出极为稀有的“异色宝可梦（Shiny Pokémon）”后代。
- role: narration
  original: However, getting a Shiny Pokémon - named for their alternate colouring - was incredibly rare. When breeding two Pokémon the chance of getting a Shiny offspring was just one in 8192.
  translation: 然而，获得一只异色宝可梦——即以不同于常态的配色著称的稀有个体——向来极其罕见。使用两只常规宝可梦培育生蛋时，孵出异色后代的概率仅有 1/8192。
- role: narration
  original: But Diamond and Pearl players noticed something odd about Pokémon traded from other countries. Try and breed one of these with a Pokémon from your game and the Shiny ratio improved considerably - to five in 8192, or about one in 1638.
  translation: 但《钻石·珍珠》的玩家们注意到一个奇怪的现象：如果用来自其他国家版本游戏通过交换获得的宝可梦来培育，把它和自己游戏里的宝可梦搭配生蛋，孵出异色宝可梦的概率会显著提升——达到 5/8192，也就是约 1/1638。
- role: narration
  original: Masuda had coded in the ratio change as a way to encourage trading with players from other countries - even though many fans would not notice any difference.
  translation: 原来，增田特意在程序里加入了这层概率提升机制，正是为了鼓励玩家与不同国家的人交换宝可梦——尽管起初许多粉丝根本不会察觉其中的差异。
- role: quote
  speaker: 增田顺一
  original: '"We really wanted to encourage trading which we''ve always had as part of the games, so we thought - how can we make promote this, how can we make people want to trade," Masuda told Eurogamer.'
  translation: “我们一直非常希望能鼓励大家去‘交换’，这也是《宝可梦》自始至终的核心玩法之一。所以我们就在思考——怎样才能推广这件事，怎样才能让大家更主动地想要互相交换呢，”增田向 Eurogamer 说道。
- role: quote
  speaker: 增田顺一
  original: '"The idea of having people get eggs which have a higher chance of being Shiny Pokémon if they are from different locations seemed like a good way to do this. Basically the idea was to spread the idea, the joy of trading in Pokémon."'
  translation: “如果把来自不同地区的宝可梦放在一起培育，生出的蛋就有更高概率孵化出异色宝可梦——这个点子看上去正是达成目标的好办法。说到底，初衷就是为了传递并推广《宝可梦》中交换的乐趣。”
- role: narration
  original: Veteran Pokémon site and forum Smogon.com is generally accepted as having noticed the mechanic first, although it wasn't until a year after Diamond and Pearl had released in Japan, and six months after their launch in Europe, that Masuda finally mentioned the mechanic on his English blog .
  translation: 通常认为，资深宝可梦站点兼论坛 Smogon.com 是最早察觉这一隐藏机制的社群；而直到《钻石·珍珠》在日本发售一年之后、在欧洲发售半年之后，增田才终于在自己的英文博客中亲口提及了这项机制。
- role: narration
  original: Still, it wasn't until years after that the mechanic had become widely known enough that he began to be asked about it in interviews.
  translation: 即便如此，又过了好几年，这套机制的知名度才广为人知到媒体开始在采访中直接向他提问的程度。
- role: narration
  original: 'By this point, the trick - which still lacked an official name - had been given a fan-made moniker: the Masuda method.'
  translation: 到了那时，这个当时还没有官方名称的秘诀，已经拥有了一个由粉丝自发取好的外号：“增田方法（Masuda Method / 国际孵化）”。
- role: quote
  speaker: 增田顺一
  original: '"I didn''t expect it to be given the name, that was a complete shock," Masuda recalled, as he remembered the time he first heard it. Masuda had been visiting the West on a press tour when a journalist brought it up.'
  translation: “我压根没想到它会用我的名字命名，当时真是吓了一跳，”增田回忆起第一次听到这个叫法时的情景。那时他正在西方媒体巡回宣传，一位记者提到了这个词。
- role: quote
  speaker: 增田顺一
  original: '"I remember someone asking about it and having to ask, ''er, what is the Masuda Method?!'' I was pretty shocked to find out."'
  translation: “我记得当时有人问起这件事，我还得反问一句‘呃，什么是增田方法？！’知道之后真的感到特别惊讶。”

  speaker: 增田顺一
  original: The name is uniquely Western, too. "the Masuda method isn't really talked about in Japan", Masuda laughed.
  translation: 这个叫法同样有着鲜明的欧美色彩。“在日本国内其实不太会有人这么叫（Masuda Method），”增田笑着说。
- role: narration
  original: Since then, the Masuda method has become a mainstay of the series, and has featured in every major Pokémon release since. The ratio of getting a Shiny Pokémon has even improved slightly (although remains low enough that Shiny creatures remain rare).
  translation: 自那以后，“增田方法”成了系列延续至今的保留机制，并在随后的每一部《宝可梦》正统作品中均有登场。后来孵出异色宝可梦的概率甚至还略微调高了一些（不过整体依旧维持在足够低的水平，以保证异色宝可梦的稀有度）。

  speaker: 增田顺一

  translation: 至于未来、以及即将发售的《宝可梦 太阳·月亮》呢？“我想大家应该可以猜到，里面多半还是会有的，”增田最后总结道。
