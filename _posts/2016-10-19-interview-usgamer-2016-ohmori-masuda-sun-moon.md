- role: narration
  original: "After spending some time with the opening hours of Pokémon Sun and Moon, I had the chance to speak with both Ohmori and long-time steward Junichi Masuda and see where they stand ahead of the new generation. As I learned, Ohmori has a very particular perspective that informs Pokémon Sun and Moon's more natural setting as well as how players interact with the game's monsters. Here are a few items that jumped out at me over the course of the interview."
  translation: 在试玩了《宝可梦 太阳·月亮》开篇数小时的内容后，我有机会与大森滋以及长期掌舵本系列的增田顺一进行了交流，了解他们在全新世代开启前的想法。通过这次访谈我发现，大森对于《宝可梦 太阳·月亮》更为贴近自然的舞台设定，以及玩家与宝可梦之间的互动方式，都有着非常独到的见解。以下是我在这场采访中印象最深的几个要点。

  translation: 大森希望《太阳·月亮》能将聚光灯对准宝可梦这些生物本身，以及训练家与它们之间的互动。你的菜单界面附着了一只爱唠叨的洛托姆——也就是洛托姆图鉴——正是这一理念的体现。不过还有一些不那么显眼的细节，比如当你第一次抱起最初的伙伴、凝望它双眼的那一刻。
- role: statement
  speaker: 大森滋
  original: "\"This is the seventh generation, but this is also my first time working as a director on an all-new generation of Pokémon games,\" said Ohmori, who joined Game Freak during the Diamond and Pearl generation. \"So what I wanted to do was reexmine Pokémon as living creatures and really focus on that relationship: how they interact with humans, how they live alongside human. Nature and abundant life were two of the major themes I wanted to focus on with Pokémon Sun and Moon. I think you'll find throughout the game that it's even more lively than previous Pokémon games.\""
  translation: “这是第七世代，同时也是我第一次担任《宝可梦》全新世代作品的总监，”在《宝可梦 钻石·珍珠》时期加入 GAME FREAK 的大森说道，“因此，我想把宝可梦重新当作活生生的生物来审视，真正聚焦于那份羁绊——它们如何与人类互动，又如何与人类共同生活。自然与丰饶的生命，是我在《宝可梦 太阳·月亮》中着重表现的两大主题。我想大家在游玩全程中都会感受到，本作比以往任何一部《宝可梦》作品都更加生机盎然。”
- role: statement
  speaker: 大森滋
  original: "As for why he's taking this approach in particular, \"I've always had an interest in showing animals in a digital medium. It's just an interest I have: I like going to the zoo, for example, and I want to show them in a more realistic way in a digital medium.\""
  translation: 至于为何唯独选择这一视角，他表示：“我一直对在数字媒介中表现动物很感兴趣。这算是我个人的一个爱好——比如我很喜欢去动物园，也希望能以更写实的方式，在数字媒介里展现它们的姿态。”

- role: narration
  original: "Game Freak takes an old-school approach to game design: Rather than poring over user feedback and advanced metrics to see what works and what doesn't, they more or less take each new generation as it arrives."
  translation: GAME FREAK 在游戏设计上秉持着相当老派的做法：他们并不会一头扎进玩家反馈和各类高阶数据指标里去复盘得失，而更像是顺势而为，把每一个新世代都当作独立的新起点来面对。
- role: statement
  speaker: 大森滋
  original: "Ohmori explains, \"I mentioned earlier, but this is the first time I've worked as the director for an entirely new generation of Pokémon, so I really had a desire to build a new game from scratch while working closely with scratch. And because introducing a new director is kind of a momentous occasion, I really wanted to use the occasion to rebuild or reexamine some things. I think trials in place of gyms is one example of that; introducing Z-Moves is also another thing. These are things I put in the game because I wanted to focus on Pokémon as living creatures, and I think these are two elements that will help express that. But just like the Pokémon themselves, each game should have their own characteristics. So it's less about looking at past games and seeing what went well or what went poorly, it's more just looking at each project as its own new thing and determining what we want to do and how we can accomplish it."
  translation: 大森解释道：“前面也提到过，这是我首次执导一个完全全新的《宝可梦》世代，所以我非常渴望从零开始打造一部新作。而且启用新总监本身算是一个颇具分量的契机，我很想借此机会重新构筑或重新审视一些既有框架。以‘考验’取代道馆便是其中一例，引入‘Z招式’也是另一项尝试。我把这些要素放进游戏，是因为我想突出宝可梦作为活生生生物的一面，而我觉得这两项设计有助于传达这一点。但就像宝可梦各自不同一样，每一部作品也都应当拥有属于自己的个性。所以与其回顾过往作品、计较哪里做得好哪里做得不好，我们更倾向于把每个项目都视作独立的新事物，先确立自己想做什么，再去思考如何实现它。”

  translation: 听到这个消息真是太好了，因为加盟大道实在太好玩了。当年玩《宝可梦 黑2·白2》的时候，我一门心思就想着尽可能多地结识其他玩家，把我所有的店铺全都升满级。看来到了《太阳·月亮》，我又要重操旧业了。
