  translation: 我们先来看一眼《胶囊怪兽》企划书的标题页。这些陌生的怪兽可能是现有宝可梦的早期设计，其中许多都经历过大幅重新设计。（后文会详细谈到。）
- original: Another possibility is that they are Pokémon that were not ultimately not included in the final games. Tajiri and Sugimori went into further detail about this aspect in an interview on Nintendo of Japan’s website. (Click the names to see the translated source for each quote.)
  translation: 另一种可能是，它们是最终未被收入正式游戏的宝可梦。田尻智和杉森建在日本任天堂网站的一次采访中更详细地谈到了这一点。（点击人名可查看每段引文的翻译出处。）
- original: “Memory capacity and deciding on a good number of characters became an issue. We actually wanted to have more. We designed around 1.5 times more characters than we actually used.”
  role: answer
  speaker: 杉森建
  translation: “内存容量和确定合适的角色数量成了问题。我们其实想做得更多。我们设计的角色数量大约是我们实际使用的1.5倍。”
- original: We designed more than 200 Pokémon for the first games, then whittled them down to 150. The way we’ve approached things is to develop whatever we want without paying any heed to how much memory is available, cutting down on Pokémon we can’t fit in later on.
  role: answer
  speaker: 田尻智
  translation: 初代游戏我们设计了200多只宝可梦，后来削减到150只。我们的做法是，不管内存有多少，先尽情开发我们想要的东西，之后再删掉装不下的宝可梦。
- original: Now let’s take a peek at the second page, a preface which outlines what Sugimori and Tajiri were hoping for Pokémon to become.
  translation: 现在我们来看第二页，这是一篇前言，概述了杉森和田尻希望宝可梦成为什么样子。

- original: A (Hypothetical) Story Set in the Not-So-Distant Future…
  quote: true
  translation: 一个（假想的）故事，背景设定在不久的将来……
- original: It’s been a month since the release of Capsule Monsters, a monster game that incorporates RPG elements into a dungeon-covered overworld. You know those vending machines that sit in front of candy stores and toy shops selling capsule toys called “gashapon”, right? I guess I could describe the game as being similar to the excitement you get from collecting those capsules.
  quote: true
  translation: 《胶囊怪兽》是一款在地下迷宫覆盖的地图上融入RPG元素的怪兽游戏，发售至今已经过了一个月。你知道糖果店和玩具店门前那些卖胶囊玩具的自动售货机吧，就是那种叫“扭蛋”的。我想，这款游戏给人的兴奋感，大概就类似于收集那些胶囊时的感觉。
- original: In Capsule Monsters, 200 types of virtual monsters live in equally virtual underground dungeons on the Game Boy. The game’s hero can befriend these monsters and win them over to his side by increasing his Charisma, a game parameter not yet widely known among Japanese audiences. Charisma is featured in the Wizardry series, but it seems like Japanese games have yet to make use of it. Naturally, Capsule Monsters also allows you to capture creatures alive rather than defeating them in battle, as you’ve done in RPGs up till now.
  quote: true
  translation: 在《胶囊怪兽》中，有200种虚拟怪兽生活在Game Boy上同样虚拟的地下迷宫里。游戏的主角可以通过提升自己的“魅力”来与这些怪兽成为朋友、将它们拉拢到自己这边，而“魅力”这一游戏参数在日本玩家中还不太为人所知。《巫术》系列中就有魅力这一项，但日本游戏似乎还没有用上它。当然，《胶囊怪兽》也允许你活捉生物，而不是像此前的RPG那样在战斗中打败它们。
  note: 《巫术》（Wizardry）是1981年起源于美国的经典RPG系列，其角色属性中包含“魅力”一项。
- original: The roster of over 200 types of monsters includes creatures that you won’t necessarily encounter, creatures who will choose not to ally with you, and creatures who you’ll miss the chance to encounter entirely, and thus never be able to catch. My friends and I call these “illusory monsters”.
  quote: true
  translation: 这200多种怪兽的名单中，有些怪兽你不一定遇得到，有些怪兽会选择不与你结盟，还有些怪兽你会完全错过相遇的机会，因此永远也抓不到。我和朋友们把这些称为“幻之怪兽”。
- original: It just so happens that I have three Green Dragons, which only appear very rarely in the corner of an underground dungeon, four levels down. In order to catch just one of these illusory monsters, players of a certain level have to spend an average of 2 hours wandering around a particular area. My friend, Takuji, doesn’t have any Green Dragons, but he is allied with 2 Fireflies instead.
  quote: true
  translation: 我碰巧有三只绿龙，它们只在地下迷宫往下第四层的角落里极少出现。要抓到这样一只幻之怪兽，达到一定等级的玩家平均得在特定区域里转上2个小时。我的朋友拓二没有绿龙，但他和2只萤火虫结了盟。
- original: In class, discussion about Capsule Monsters really heats up after the bell rings. Who has which illusory characters? How many? We’re not just bragging, exactly. Once my friends and I come to an agreement, we connect our Game Boys via Link Cable and then we can use it to trade monsters. That’s why we get excited, because we’re negotiating which monsters to trade for what, and how many.
  quote: true
  translation: 在学校里，下课铃一响，关于《胶囊怪兽》的讨论就热烈起来。谁有哪只幻之角色？有几只？我们倒也不完全是在炫耀。我和朋友们一旦谈妥，就用连接线把各自的Game Boy连起来，然后就能用它来交换怪兽。我们之所以兴奋，是因为我们在商量用哪只怪兽换哪只、换几只。
- original: Takuji and I finally finished negotiating. He’d agreed to trade one of his Fireflies for two of my Green Dragons. But I still felt like our exchange was a little unbalanced. I asked if he wouldn’t mind adding 5 Powerkings, monsters with the strength you might expect to see in soldiers, and we finally struck a bargain. Powerkings have a high encounter rate, and you’ll quickly run into them if you wander around dungeons. They’re strong, though, and the theory is that, if you have a lot of them, you can put them to work as soldiers when you run into other monsters. Takuji and I hooked up the Link Cable and traded monsters. The Game Boy plays sound effects as the monster data is being transferred, so we could hear the monsters’ cries.
  quote: true
  translation: 我和拓二终于谈妥了。他同意用他的一只萤火虫换我的两只绿龙。但我还是觉得这笔交换有点不平衡。我问他能不能再加上5只力量王——那种强度差不多相当于士兵的怪兽，我们最终成交了。力量王的遭遇率很高，在迷宫里转一转很快就会碰上。不过它们很强，理论上，如果你有很多只，遇到其他怪兽时就能把它们当士兵来用。我和拓二接上连接线，交换了怪兽。传输怪兽数据时Game Boy会播放音效，所以我们能听到怪兽的叫声。
- original: Word of mouth has turned Capsule Monsters into a hit. While I was riding the train to school, I discovered that students from other schools played too. I pulled my Link Cable out of my school bag as I addressed them. “Excuse me,” I said. “I have a Firefly and a Green Dragon. What do you guys have? How about a trade?”
  quote: true
  translation: 口口相传让《胶囊怪兽》成了热门。我坐电车上学时，发现其他学校的学生也在玩。我从书包里掏出连接线，对他们说：“打扰一下，我有一只萤火虫和一只绿龙。你们有什么？要不要交换？”

- original: At first, the game was more RPG-like. It was only in the middle of development that the goal of the game became completing your Pokédex.
  role: answer
  speaker: 杉森建
  translation: 起初，这款游戏更像RPG。直到开发中期，游戏的目标才变成完成你的宝可梦图鉴 App。
- original: You also may have noticed that the design proposal makes no mention of two separate versions. This is because the idea for the split originated later in development, on the recommendation of Miyamoto himself.
  translation: 你可能也注意到，这份设计提案没有提到两个不同的版本。这是因为分成双版本的构想是后来在开发过程中才出现的，而且是宫本茂本人建议的。
- original: In the beginning, the original Pokémon didn’t have different variations such as Red and Blue. I took the advice of Miyamoto and split it into two, something that was a first for us.
  role: answer
  speaker: 田尻智
  translation: 最初，原本的宝可梦并没有红版和蓝版这样的不同版本。我采纳了宫本茂的建议，把它分成两个版本，这对我们来说是头一回。
- original: As for Miyamoto’s reasons, well…
  translation: 至于宫本茂的理由，嗯……
- original: I didn’t suggest splitting the games because it would allow us to sell more copies of the same thing. I just thought it would be more fun for the players if, say, there were 3 siblings and they all owned something unique. That way, they’d be able to communicate. I didn’t want to release separate versions of the games just to increase the marketability.
  role: answer
  speaker: 宫本茂
  translation: 我建议把游戏分成两个版本，并不是因为这样能让我们把同样的东西卖出更多份。我只是觉得，假如一家有三个兄弟姐妹，每个人各拥有一样独有的东西，那对玩家来说会更有趣。这样一来，他们就能互相交流。我并不想仅仅为了提高销路而发售不同的版本。
