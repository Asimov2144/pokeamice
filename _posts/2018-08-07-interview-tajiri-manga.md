- type: paragraph
  original: On May 17th, 2018, Game Freak designer James Turner (the creator of Pokémon like Vanillite, Golurk, and Poipole) tweeted pictures of a few excerpts from the then-recently published manga about Satoshi Tajiri’s life and the creation of Game Freak. Interestingly enough, despite claims from the developers that most of the documentation about the first two Pokémon games either never existed or was lost to time, one of the panels in this manga contains a look at an internal poll during development.
  translation: 2018年5月17日，GAME FREAK的设计师詹姆斯·特纳（James Turner，宝可梦迷你冰、泥偶巨人、毒贝比等的创造者）在推特上发布了几张当时刚出版的关于田尻智生平及GAME FREAK创立历程的漫画的节选图片。有趣的是，尽管开发者们声称关于前两部宝可梦游戏的大部分文档要么从未存在，要么已随时间遗失，但这部漫画的其中一个分镜却展示了开发期间的一次内部投票。
  note: 詹姆斯·特纳是英国设计师，参与多款宝可梦游戏设计。
- type: paragraph
  original: Our curiosity piqued, the Helix Chamber team – made up of translators, coders, people who know random trivia about the franchise, nerds, magnification wizards, you name it – gathered together, obtained a copy of the manga, scanned everything, and were able to unearth quite a lot of information.
  translation: 我们的好奇心被激起，Helix Chamber团队——由翻译、程序员、了解该系列各种冷知识的人、极客、图像放大专家等组成——聚集在一起，获取了这本漫画的副本，扫描了所有内容，并挖掘出了相当多的信息。
  note: Helix Chamber 是一个粉丝研究团队，以挖掘宝可梦游戏数据闻名。

  original: Along with the Pokémon charts, there’s also a table with votes and a results sheet (B). All three of these documents were made for one of the several popularity polls (人気投票) that Game Freak held periodically in order to rate their Pokémon designs and help decide which monsters would be in the final game.
  translation: 除了宝可梦图表外，还有一个投票表格和结果单（B）。这三份文件都是为GAME FREAK定期举办的多次人气投票（人気投票）之一而制作的，目的是评估他们的宝可梦设计，并帮助决定哪些怪物会出现在最终游戏中。
  note: 人気投票是日本常见的粉丝投票活动，此处指公司内部投票。

- type: paragraph
  original: As a side note, in the final RG games, there are several sprites which don’t look like they were made directly by any individual credited artist. Instead, they look rather hastily scaled down . There is some evidence supporting that they did this within the poll; someone thought to re-proportion Pokémon who had received an evolution later in development. These sprites in the final games were already pretty blatantly rescaled, even before the evidence was solid enough. Some others were at least re-polished or re-drawn from scratch, like Venonat and Krabby. We also have evidence that a couple of bigger sprites were reworked into their respective evolutions (Doduo, Krabby).
  translation: 顺便提一下，在最终的《红/绿》游戏中，有几个精灵图看起来并非由任何署名艺术家直接绘制，而是显得像是匆忙缩小尺寸的。有证据支持他们在这次投票中这样做了；有人想到为在开发后期获得进化的宝可梦重新调整比例。即使在证据充分之前，最终游戏中的这些精灵图就已经相当明显地重新缩放过了。其他一些至少经过了重新润色或从头重绘，比如毛球和大钳蟹。我们还有证据表明，一些较大的精灵图被重制为它们各自的进化形态（嘟嘟、大钳蟹）。

- type: paragraph
  original: Block 1 doesn’t follow or even use index numbers. Instead, Pokémon are marked with a circled number that differs from the order in which they were programmed. These numbers instead match up with a different data list in the game, that being the cry data index . Each cry base (or melody) has a different hex value in the code. For example, Nidoran’s is 0x00 (decimal 0), Spearow’s is 0x10 (decimal 16), and Rhydon’s is 0x11 (decimal 17).
  translation: 第1块并不遵循甚至不使用索引编号。相反，宝可梦被标记了带圆圈的编号，这与它们被编程的顺序不同。这些编号对应游戏中的另一个数据列表，即叫声数据索引。每个叫声基础（或旋律）在代码中都有不同的十六进制值。例如，尼多朗的为0x00（十进制0），烈雀的为0x10（十进制16），钻角犀兽的为0x11（十进制17）。
