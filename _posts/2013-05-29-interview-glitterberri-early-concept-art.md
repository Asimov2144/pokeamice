- original: In 1989, two events happened that were important for the development of Pokémon. First off, Nintendo released the Game Boy. 5 days later, future Pokémon developers Satoshi Tajiri and Ken Sugimori turned Game Freak, their self-published gaming fanzine, into a video game company.
  translation: 1989年发生了两件对宝可梦开发至关重要的事。首先，任天堂推出了Game Boy。5天后，未来的宝可梦开发者田尻智和杉森建将他们自行出版的游戏同人志Game Freak变成了一家电子游戏公司。
- type: image
  image: /assets/img/interviews/2013-05-29-interview-glitterberri-early-concept-art/002.jpg
  alt: Early Concept Art
- original: The Game Boy was originally seen as a system geared towards puzzle and action games. Indeed, the handheld saw many releases that fell into these categories. Then came the release of a Square RPG known in English as The Final Fantasy Legend. When Tajiri saw how successful it was, he realized that even non-action games were possible on a portable system.
  translation: Game Boy最初被视为一款面向益智和动作游戏的系统。事实上，这款掌机上确实发布了许多属于这些类别的作品。后来，Square的一款RPG——英文名为The Final Fantasy Legend——发售了。当田尻智看到它如此成功时，他意识到即使在便携系统上，非动作游戏也是可行的。
  note: The Final Fantasy Legend即《最终幻想传奇》，在日本名为《魔界塔士Sa·Ga》。
- type: image
  image: /assets/img/interviews/2013-05-29-interview-glitterberri-early-concept-art/003.jpg
  alt: Early Concept Art
- original: What Tajiri really had his eye on, however, was the Game Boy’s linking functionality. This data transfer system inspired the developer to dream up a game in which players would be able to trade with each other. Tajiri was also a fan of Ultra Seven, a TV fantasy series that was a followup to Ultraman, from which he got the idea of capsule monsters. He adopted the name Capsule Monsters for the RPG he set out to plan. The game, he imagined, would have monsters in containers similar to capsule toys travelling back and forth via the Game Link Cable. In the fall of 1990, Tajiri brought the project proposal he’d created to Nintendo, who decided to fund its development.
  translation: 然而，田尻智真正看中的是Game Boy的联机功能。这种数据传输系统激发了这位开发者的梦想：一款玩家能够彼此交换的游戏。田尻智也是《奥特赛文》的粉丝，这部电视奇幻剧是《奥特曼》的续作，他从其中获得了胶囊怪兽的点子。他为自己着手策划的RPG采用了“胶囊怪兽”这个名字。他设想，游戏中的怪兽会像胶囊玩具一样装在容器里，通过Game Link Cable来回传输。1990年秋天，田尻智将他制作的项目企划书带到了任天堂，任天堂决定资助其开发。
  note: 《奥特赛文》是1967年播出的特摄剧，为《奥特曼》的续作。
- type: image
  image: /assets/img/interviews/2013-05-29-interview-glitterberri-early-concept-art/004.jpg
  alt: Early Concept Art
- original: Tajiri was unable to trademark the name Capsule Monsters due to copyright issues, and the shortened version of the game’s name, Capumon, just didn’t sound very nice. Accordingly, he revised the title to Pocket Monsters, which was then shortened to “Pokémon”.
  translation: 由于版权问题，田尻智无法将“胶囊怪兽”注册为商标，而游戏名称的缩写版“Capumon”听起来又不太好。因此，他将标题改为“Pocket Monsters”，后来缩写为“Pokémon”。

  translation: 我们先来看一眼《胶囊怪兽》企划书的标题页。这些陌生的怪兽可能是现有宝可梦的早期设计，其中许多后来都经历了大幅重新设计。（详情后述。）
  note: 《胶囊怪兽》（Capsule Monsters）是宝可梦系列开发阶段的早期企划名称。
- original: Another possibility is that they are Pokémon that were not ultimately not included in the final games. Tajiri and Sugimori went into further detail about this aspect in an interview on Nintendo of Japan’s website. (Click the names to see the translated source for each quote.)
  translation: 另一种可能是，它们是最终未被收录进正式游戏的宝可梦。田尻智和杉森建在日本任天堂网站的一次采访中对此有更详细的说明。（点击人名可查看每段引文的翻译出处。）
- original: “Memory capacity and deciding on a good number of characters became an issue. We actually wanted to have more. We designed around 1.5 times more characters than we actually used.”
  role: answer
  speaker: 杉森建
  translation: “内存容量和确定合适的角色数量成了问题。我们其实想做得更多。我们设计的角色数量大约是我们实际使用的1.5倍。”
- original: We designed more than 200 Pokémon for the first games, then whittled them down to 150. The way we’ve approached things is to develop whatever we want without paying any heed to how much memory is available, cutting down on Pokémon we can’t fit in later on.
  role: answer
  speaker: 田尻智
  translation: 我们在初代游戏中设计了200多只宝可梦，然后削减到150只。我们的做法是，先不管内存够不够，想做什么就做什么，之后再砍掉塞不进去的宝可梦。
- original: Now let’s take a peek at the second page, a preface which outlines what Sugimori and Tajiri were hoping for Pokémon to become.
  translation: 现在我们来看第二页，这是一篇前言，概述了杉森建和田尻智希望宝可梦成为什么样子。

  translation: 设计前提
- type: image
  image: /assets/img/interviews/2013-05-29-interview-glitterberri-early-concept-art/007.jpg
  alt: Early Concept Art
  caption: Pocket Monsters was named Capsule Monsters in the original planning documents. These include a prediction written by Tajiri in story-form regarding how the game would be received and played after its release.
- original: A (Hypothetical) Story Set in the Not-So-Distant Future…
  quote: true
  translation: 一个（假想的）发生在不远未来的故事……
- original: It’s been a month since the release of Capsule Monsters, a monster game that incorporates RPG elements into a dungeon-covered overworld. You know those vending machines that sit in front of candy stores and toy shops selling capsule toys called “gashapon”, right? I guess I could describe the game as being similar to the excitement you get from collecting those capsules.
  quote: true
  translation: 《胶囊怪兽》发售已经一个月了，这款怪兽游戏将RPG元素融入了遍布地下城的俯视地图。你知道糖果店和玩具店门前那些卖扭蛋胶囊玩具的自动售货机吧？我想，这款游戏带来的兴奋感，大概就类似于收集那些胶囊时的感觉。
  note: “扭蛋”（gashapon）指投币后随机掉出胶囊玩具的自动售货机。
- original: In Capsule Monsters, 200 types of virtual monsters live in equally virtual underground dungeons on the Game Boy. The game’s hero can befriend these monsters and win them over to his side by increasing his Charisma, a game parameter not yet widely known among Japanese audiences. Charisma is featured in the Wizardry series, but it seems like Japanese games have yet to make use of it. Naturally, Capsule Monsters also allows you to capture creatures alive rather than defeating them in battle, as you’ve done in RPGs up till now.
  quote: true
  translation: 在《胶囊怪兽》中，200种虚拟怪兽生活在Game Boy上同样虚拟的地下迷宫里。游戏的主角可以通过提升自己的“魅力”来与这些怪兽交朋友、把它们拉拢到自己这边——“魅力”这一游戏参数当时在日本玩家中还不太为人所知。《巫术》系列中有“魅力”这一设定，但日本游戏似乎还没有用上它。当然，《胶囊怪兽》也允许你活捉生物，而不是像此前的RPG那样在战斗中击败它们。
  note: 《巫术》（Wizardry）是早期的西方地牢探索类RPG系列，对日本RPG影响很大。
- original: The roster of over 200 types of monsters includes creatures that you won’t necessarily encounter, creatures who will choose not to ally with you, and creatures who you’ll miss the chance to encounter entirely, and thus never be able to catch. My friends and I call these “illusory monsters”.
  quote: true
  translation: 超过200种怪物的阵容中，包含了你未必会遇到的生物、会选择不与你结盟的生物，以及你完全错失相遇机会、因而永远无法捕捉的生物。我和朋友们称这些为“幻影怪物”。
- original: It just so happens that I have three Green Dragons, which only appear very rarely in the corner of an underground dungeon, four levels down. In order to catch just one of these illusory monsters, players of a certain level have to spend an average of 2 hours wandering around a particular area. My friend, Takuji, doesn’t have any Green Dragons, but he is allied with 2 Fireflies instead.
  quote: true
  translation: 碰巧我有三只绿龙，它们极少出现在地下城深处第四层的角落里。为了捕捉到这样一只幻影怪物，一定等级的玩家平均需要在特定区域徘徊2小时。我的朋友Takuji没有绿龙，但他与2只萤火虫结盟了。
- original: In class, discussion about Capsule Monsters really heats up after the bell rings. Who has which illusory characters? How many? We’re not just bragging, exactly. Once my friends and I come to an agreement, we connect our Game Boys via Link Cable and then we can use it to trade monsters. That’s why we get excited, because we’re negotiating which monsters to trade for what, and how many.
  quote: true
  translation: 课堂上，铃声响起后，关于胶囊怪兽的讨论才真正热烈起来。谁拥有哪些幻影角色？有多少？我们并不只是在炫耀。一旦我和朋友们达成一致，我们就会通过连接线连接我们的Game Boy，然后就可以用它来交换怪物。这就是我们兴奋的原因，因为我们正在协商用什么怪物交换什么，以及数量多少。
- original: Takuji and I finally finished negotiating. He’d agreed to trade one of his Fireflies for two of my Green Dragons. But I still felt like our exchange was a little unbalanced. I asked if he wouldn’t mind adding 5 Powerkings, monsters with the strength you might expect to see in soldiers, and we finally struck a bargain. Powerkings have a high encounter rate, and you’ll quickly run into them if you wander around dungeons. They’re strong, though, and the theory is that, if you have a lot of them, you can put them to work as soldiers when you run into other monsters. Takuji and I hooked up the Link Cable and traded monsters. The Game Boy plays sound effects as the monster data is being transferred, so we could hear the monsters’ cries.
  quote: true
  translation: Takuji和我终于完成了协商。他同意用他的一只萤火虫换我的两只绿龙。但我仍然觉得我们的交换有点不平衡。我问他是否介意再加上5只力量王——这种怪物的力量就像士兵一样——我们最终达成了协议。力量王的遭遇率很高，如果你在地下城四处走动，很快就会遇到它们。不过它们很强，理论上，如果你有很多，当你遇到其他怪物时，可以把它们当作士兵来使用。Takuji和我连接了连接线并交换了怪物。Game Boy在传输怪物数据时会播放音效，所以我们可以听到怪物的叫声。
- original: Word of mouth has turned Capsule Monsters into a hit. While I was riding the train to school, I discovered that students from other schools played too. I pulled my Link Cable out of my school bag as I addressed them. “Excuse me,” I said. “I have a Firefly and a Green Dragon. What do you guys have? How about a trade?”
  quote: true
  translation: 口碑让胶囊怪兽大受欢迎。当我坐火车去学校时，我发现其他学校的学生也在玩。我从书包里拿出连接线，对他们说：“打扰一下，我有一只萤火虫和一只绿龙。你们有什么？交换怎么样？”

- original: 'Regarding the type of gameplay that was intended for Capsule Monsters, Sugimori had this to say:'
  translation: 关于胶囊怪兽原本设想的游戏玩法类型，杉森建是这样说的：
- original: At first, the game was more RPG-like. It was only in the middle of development that the goal of the game became completing your Pokédex.
  role: answer
  speaker: 杉森建
  translation: 起初，游戏更像RPG。直到开发中期，游戏的目标才变成完成你的宝可梦图鉴 App。
- original: You also may have noticed that the design proposal makes no mention of two separate versions. This is because the idea for the split originated later in development, on the recommendation of Miyamoto himself.
  translation: 你可能也注意到了，设计提案中没有提到两个不同的版本。这是因为分版本的想法是在开发后期，根据宫本茂本人的建议才产生的。

  role: answer
  speaker: 田尻智
  translation: 一开始，最初的宝可梦并没有红和蓝这样的不同版本。我接受了宫本茂的建议，将其分成两个版本，这对我们来说是第一次。
- original: As for Miyamoto’s reasons, well…
  translation: 至于宫本茂的理由，嗯……
- original: I didn’t suggest splitting the games because it would allow us to sell more copies of the same thing. I just thought it would be more fun for the players if, say, there were 3 siblings and they all owned something unique. That way, they’d be able to communicate. I didn’t want to release separate versions of the games just to increase the marketability.
  role: answer
  speaker: 宫本茂
  translation: 我提议将游戏分成两个版本，并不是为了能卖出更多份相同的东西。我只是觉得，如果比如说有3个兄弟姐妹，他们各自拥有一些独特的东西，这样会更有趣。这样一来，他们就能互相交流了。我并不想仅仅为了提高市场销量而发行不同的游戏版本。

  alt: Early Concept Art
- original: Lapras was obviously an example of the latter. The idea of Pokémon living and working alongside humans isn’t really fully fleshed out in the first generation of games. The only Pokémon seen performing a role other than that of a battling monster or pet is the Machop helping out with construction in Vermillion City. Lapras’ design also went through some minor changes, with the Pokémon gaining ears and losing some of its resemblance to the Loch Ness monster.
  translation: 拉普拉斯显然是后者的例子。宝可梦与人类共同生活和工作的想法在第一代游戏中并没有完全展开。除了作为战斗怪物或宠物之外，唯一看到扮演其他角色的宝可梦是在枯叶市帮忙建设的腕力。拉普拉斯的设计也经历了一些小改动，这只宝可梦长出了耳朵，并且不再那么像尼斯湖水怪。
