  original: One of the interviews in Pokemon Peer discusses the new Pokemon designs, and Bangiras has spent the last several hours translating most of it (she was too tired to finish, so we'll add the rest later). The graphic designers discuss how they designed the starter Pokemon, and much of what they say, in my opinion, supports the validity of the evolutions posted the other day. Check it out! It's very interesting.
  translation: 《宝可梦同行》中的一篇访谈讨论了新宝可梦的设计，Bangiras 花了几个小时翻译了大部分内容（她太累了没翻完，我们稍后会补上剩余部分）。美术设计师们讨论了如何设计初始宝可梦，在我看来，他们所说的大部分内容都支持了前几天发布的进化形态的合理性。快来看看吧！非常有趣。
  note: Bangiras 为译者网名；'starter Pokemon' 指初始宝可梦；'evolutions' 指进化形态。
- type: paragraph
  original: Today we’re meeting with all of the graphic designers, who can be considered the parents of Pokemon.  To start, please tell us how you are involved with the Pocket Monsters series.
  translation: 今天我们采访了所有美术设计师，他们可以说是宝可梦的父母。首先，请告诉我们你们是如何参与《宝可梦》系列的。
  note: Pocket Monsters 为宝可梦日文原名，此处译为宝可梦。
- type: paragraph
  original: 'Sugimori:  I’m in charge as the 2D art director.  The GM of design.  I supervise the dot art (sprite graphics displayed on the game screen). I’ve been involved with the Pocket Monsters series since its inception, so I also supervised the design of the original human and Pokemon characters.  This time I was slightly less involved, but I did work on the hero and Pokemon designs.'
  translation: 杉森建：我担任2D美术总监，是设计方面的总经理。我负责监督点阵图（游戏画面中显示的精灵图）。我从《宝可梦》系列之初就参与其中，所以也监督了最初的人类和宝可梦角色的设计。这次我参与度稍低，但确实参与了主角和宝可梦的设计。
  speaker: 杉森建
  note: 杉森建为宝可梦系列长期美术总监；'GM' 可能为 'General Manager' 缩写。
- type: paragraph
  original: 'Oumura:  I’m in charge of 2D graphics. I started with Pocket Monsters Diamond and Pearl, and this time I worked on the designs of the human characters and Pokemon such as Mijumaru.'
  translation: 大村：我负责2D图形。我从《宝可梦 钻石／珍珠》开始参与，这次我负责人物和宝可梦的设计，比如水水獭。
  speaker: 大村祐介
  note: Mijumaru 为水水獭的日文名，官方中文名为水水獭。
- type: paragraph
  original: 'Sugimori: Ibe and Tagami came on board at Game Freak when we were working on Pocket Monsters Platinum.  This is the first time they’ve made a game from scratch.'
  translation: 杉森建：伊部和多上是在我们开发《宝可梦 白金》时加入Game Freak的。这是他们第一次从零开始制作游戏。
  speaker: 杉森建
  note: Ibe 和 Tagami 为日文姓氏，此处音译为伊部和多上；Game Freak 为开发公司。
- type: paragraph
  original: 'Ibe: Right.  The Pokemon I was in charge of designing was Victini.  Aside from that, I designed about 10 other Pokemon.'
  translation: 伊部：是的。我负责设计的宝可梦是比克提尼。除此之外，我还设计了大约10只其他宝可梦。
  speaker: 井部真那
  note: Victini 官方译名为比克提尼。
- type: paragraph
  original: 'Tagami: I designed Tsutaaja. After that, the Little Pigeon Pokemon Mamepato, and a Bug-type Pokemon called Kurumiru, for example.  I worked on about 10 Pokemon.'
  translation: 多上：我设计了藤藤蛇。之后还有小鸽子宝可梦豆豆鸽，以及虫属性的虫宝包等等。我设计了大约10只宝可梦。
  speaker: 田上玲子
  note: Tsutaaja 为藤藤蛇日文名，Mamepato 为豆豆鸽日文名，Kurumiru 为虫宝包日文名。
- type: paragraph
  original: Between the 4 of you are everything from veterans to newcomers, but how many designers worked on Pocket Monsters Black and White in total?
  translation: 你们四人中有老手也有新人，那么总共有多少设计师参与了《宝可梦 黑／白》的制作？
- type: paragraph
  original: 'Sugimori: There were 17 people total.  Each person designed about 10 Pokemon. [Note: 17x10 = 170!]'
  translation: 杉森建：总共有17人。每人设计了大约10只宝可梦。[注：17×10=170！]
  speaker: 杉森建
  note: 原文中的注释保留，说明数量。
- type: paragraph
  original: This time all of the Pokemon are new.  When did you start designing the Pokemon?
  translation: 这次所有宝可梦都是全新的。你们是什么时候开始设计这些宝可梦的？
- type: paragraph
  original: 'Oumura: Around the Spring of 2008.'
  translation: 大村：大约在2008年春天。
  speaker: 大村祐介
- type: paragraph
  original: 'Sugimori: We were developing it at the same time as Pocket Monsters Platinum and Pocket Monsters Heart Gold and Soul Silver.  In this game, since old Pokemon do not appear, and a new world is the stage, we needed to create an ecosystem purely for the newly-designed Pokemon.  Up until now, we designed with the philosophy that, “a mouse-like Pokemon already exists, so let’s make other Pokemon”—but this time they were all new.  So, once again we designed a mouse-like Pokemon, a dog-like Pokemon, etc. from scratch.'
  translation: 杉森建：我们是在开发《宝可梦 白金》和《宝可梦 心金／魂银》的同时进行开发的。在这款游戏中，由于旧宝可梦不会出现，而且舞台是一个新世界，我们需要为全新设计的宝可梦创造一个纯粹的生态系统。到目前为止，我们的设计理念是“已经有类似老鼠的宝可梦了，所以我们来做其他宝可梦”——但这次它们都是全新的。所以，我们再次从零开始设计了类似老鼠的宝可梦、类似狗的宝可梦等等。
  speaker: 杉森建
- type: paragraph
  original: Then, you started from nothing in the true sense of the word.  What kind of Pokemon did you think of?
  translation: 那么，你们真的是从零开始的。你们构思了什么样的宝可梦呢？
- type: paragraph
  original: 'Sugimori: At first I told the designers, “You can draw freely; draw things you like.” Gradually, the number of Pokemon was decided, and the instructions became more concrete, such as, “We don’t have enough of this Type, can you try to draw some?”  By the end they were directly ordered, “Draw this Pokemon!”'
  translation: 杉森建：起初我对设计师们说：“你们可以自由发挥，画自己喜欢的东西。”渐渐地，宝可梦的数量确定了，指示也变得更加具体，比如“我们缺少这种属性，你能试着画一些吗？”到最后，他们直接接到命令：“画这只宝可梦！”
  speaker: 杉森建
- type: paragraph
  original: 'Ibe: I like dogs, so I submitted a dog-like Pokemon.'
  translation: 伊部：我喜欢狗，所以我提交了一只像狗的宝可梦。
  speaker: 井部真那
- type: paragraph
  original: 'Sugimori: Right. She likes dogs.  The Pokemon designs are a collection of the individual designers’ emotional attachments.  That’s why I originally said, “Why don’t you draw the things you like?”'
  translation: 杉森建：没错，她喜欢狗。宝可梦的设计是设计师们个人情感寄托的集合。这就是为什么我最初说：“你们为什么不画自己喜欢的东西呢？”
  speaker: 杉森建
- type: paragraph
  original: 'Tagami: In my case, it’s bugs.  There really is an insect that wads up leaves, called an Otoshibumi.  I thought that behavior was fascinating, so I tried to design a Pokemon based on it.'
  translation: 田上：对我来说，是虫子。确实有一种昆虫会把叶子卷起来，叫做“卷叶虫”。我觉得那种行为很有趣，所以我试着设计一只基于它的宝可梦。
  speaker: 田上玲子
  note: Otoshibumi 可能指卷叶象鼻虫，一种会卷叶产卵的昆虫。
- type: paragraph
  original: 'Oumura: I looked at the lineup of Pokemon that everyone had submitted to me, and designed whatever was missing.  Piplup from Pocket Monsters Diamond and Pearl was embraced, and this time I was once again designing Water-type Pokemon.  Not to suggest that Water-types are my specialty.'
  translation: 大村：我看了大家提交给我的宝可梦阵容，然后设计缺失的部分。宝可梦钻石·珍珠中的波加曼很受欢迎，这次我又设计了水属性的宝可梦。并不是说水属性是我的专长。
  speaker: 大村祐介
- type: paragraph
  original: Mr. Oumura was Mijumaru, Mr. Tagami was Tsutaaja.  You designed the 3 Starter Pokemon.
  translation: 大村先生负责水水獭，田上先生负责藤藤蛇。你们设计了这三只初始宝可梦。
  note: Mijumaru 即水水獭，Tsutaaja 即藤藤蛇，均为宝可梦黑白中的初始宝可梦。
- type: paragraph
  original: 'Sugimori:  The 3 Starter Pokemon and the Legendary Pokemon were a lot of trouble.'
  translation: 杉森建：三只初始宝可梦和传说中的宝可梦费了不少功夫。
  speaker: 杉森建
- type: paragraph
  original: 'Tagami:  A lot of trouble.  I think of Tsutaaja all the time.'
  translation: 田上：确实费了不少功夫。我一直在想藤藤蛇。
  speaker: 田上玲子
- type: paragraph
  original: 'Sugimori:  Of course, it was determined that the 3 Starters would be Grass, Fire, and Water-type Pokemon.  We’ll combine the shared horror of our experiences up until now.  The Water-type and dog weren’t cute, the Grass-type and ??? looked scary [note: we''ll fix this sentence later]. (laughs).  This time, a designer who isn’t here presented the Fire-type as a pig, and Mr. Tagami  presented the Grass-type Pokemon as a snake.  I decided it was an interesting combination.'
  translation: 杉森建：当然，三只初始宝可梦被确定为草、火、水属性。我们将结合至今为止的共同恐怖经历。水属性和狗不可爱，草属性和？？？看起来很可怕。（笑）这次，一位不在场的设计师把火属性设计成了猪，田上先生把草属性设计成了蛇。我觉得这是个有趣的组合。
  speaker: 杉森建
  note: 原文中“???”可能指某种属性或宝可梦，此处保留原文。
- type: paragraph
  original: 'Tagami: I was reading a picture book and it said, “There are snakes that look like vines.”  I said, “That’s it!” (laughs)  It was a hint at an idea.'
  translation: 田上：我在读一本图画书，上面写着：“有看起来像藤蔓的蛇。”我说：“就是它了！”（笑）这给了我一个灵感。
  speaker: 田上玲子
- type: paragraph
  original: 'Sugimori: I told him that snakes are discomforting.  “When you see the line entering its stomach, it''s creepy."'
  translation: 杉森建：我告诉他蛇让人不舒服。“当你看到那条线进入它的肚子时，很吓人。”
  speaker: 杉森建
- type: paragraph
  original: 'Tagami: But being discomforting is a snake’s most important trait.  I was conscious of that with Tsutaaja as well.'
  translation: 田上：但让人不舒服是蛇最重要的特征。在设计藤藤蛇时我也意识到了这一点。
  speaker: 田上玲子
- type: paragraph
  original: 'Sugimori: Since it was discomforting, if we took that away, it would become a character without any personality.'
  translation: 杉森建：既然它让人不舒服，如果我们去掉这一点，它就会变成一个没有个性的角色。
  speaker: 杉森建
- type: paragraph
  original: You combined the elements of the Grass-type and the snake to create a Pokemon.
  translation: 你们结合了草属性和蛇的元素，创造了一只宝可梦。
- type: paragraph
  original: 'Sugimori:  Next we needed to give it a personality.  It will be boring if all 3 Starters have the same personality, so we pick a direction for the personality, such as Naughty or Gentle.  This time, the pig Pokemon was Naughty,  and it had a reckless feel.  Tsutaaja was aristocratic….for example like “The Rose of Versailles”. (laughs)'
  translation: 杉森建：接下来我们需要赋予它个性。如果三只初始宝可梦个性相同会很无聊，所以我们会为个性选择一个方向，比如“顽皮”或“温顺”。这次，猪宝可梦是“顽皮”的，给人一种鲁莽的感觉。藤藤蛇则很有贵族气质……比如像《凡尔赛玫瑰》那样。（笑）
  speaker: 杉森建
  note: 《凡尔赛玫瑰》是一部以法国大革命为背景的日本漫画，主角奥斯卡具有贵族气质。
- type: paragraph
  original: 'Tagami: I created an image like something out of an art museum.  Like the Hermitage Museum in Russia. (laughs)'
  translation: 田上：我构思了一个像美术馆一样的形象，比如俄罗斯的冬宫博物馆。（笑）
  speaker: 田上玲子
  note: 田上（Tagami）是宝可梦设计师之一。冬宫博物馆（Hermitage Museum）是世界著名博物馆，此处比喻设计灵感来源。
- type: paragraph
  original: 'Sugimori: This time, the Water-type was the most troublesome.'
  translation: 杉森：这次，水属性是最棘手的。
  speaker: 杉森建
  note: 杉森（Sugimori）指杉森建，宝可梦系列艺术总监。
- type: paragraph
  original: 'Oomura: I said, “How about a sea otter for the Water-type?”  But if a sea otter were to evolve, what would it become? That’s where I hit a wall.  Eventually I said that if a sea otter was to evolve, it would transform into something completely different. [Note: The third rumored evolution of Mijumaru from a few days ago looks completely different than Mijumaru.]'
  translation: 大村：我说过“水属性用海獭怎么样？”但如果海獭进化，它会变成什么？我在这里卡住了。最后我说，如果海獭要进化，它会变成完全不同的东西。[注：几天前传闻的球海獭的第三阶段进化看起来与球海獭完全不同。]
  speaker: 大村祐介
  note: 大村（Oomura）是设计师。球海獭（Mijumaru）是第五世代水属性御三家。原文中的注是访谈者加的，翻译时保留。
- type: paragraph
  original: 'Sugimori: Since we intended for those 3 starter Pokemon to be with the hero throughout the game, we wanted them to have surprising evolutions. We kept adding more twists so the forms of the third-stage Evolutions would have an impact.'
  translation: 杉森：因为我们打算让这三只初学者宝可梦在整个游戏中陪伴主角，所以我们希望它们有令人惊讶的进化。我们不断加入更多转折，让第三阶段的进化形态具有冲击力。
  speaker: 杉森建
- type: paragraph
  original: 'Oomura: Also, this time we wanted the 3 starter Pokemon to be split into Japanese, Western, and Chinese imagery. We said Tsutaaja would represent Western imagery, Pokabu Chinese, and Mijumaru Japanese. We discussed it: "Couldn''t a sea otter be a samurai?"'
  translation: 大村：另外，这次我们想让三只初学者宝可梦分别体现日式、西式和中式的意象。我们决定藤藤蛇代表西方意象，暖暖猪代表中国，球海獭代表日本。我们讨论过：“海獭难道不能是武士吗？”
  speaker: 大村祐介
  note: 藤藤蛇（Tsutaaja）、暖暖猪（Pokabu）、球海獭（Mijumaru）为第五世代御三家。

  original: 'Oomura: I was a little worried, so I went to watch the sea otters at the aquarium.  I happened to see the sea lion show, I noticed how strong the sea lions were.  I thought, ah, I’ll try combining sea lions and otters together.  I came up with the idea of them fighting by using the shells on their stomachs like swords, and Mijumaru and its [next] evolution were completed.'
  translation: 大村：我有点担心，所以去水族馆观察海獭。碰巧看到了海狮表演，我注意到海狮非常强壮。我想，啊，我试着把海狮和海獭结合起来吧。我想到了它们用肚子上的贝壳像剑一样战斗的点子，球海獭和它的[下一个]进化就这样完成了。

- type: paragraph
  original: 'Sugimori:  At the beginning of development, the entire team went to the zoo.  In this case it was the Tama Zoo that we visited. We each went our own way to watch the animals.  The mole exhibit was really interesting.  Tubes were connected through the ceiling, and the moles ran through them at incredible speeds.  I was shocked.'
  translation: 杉森：在开发初期，整个团队去了动物园。这次我们去的是多摩动物园。我们各自分散去观察动物。鼹鼠展区非常有趣。管子连接在天花板上，鼹鼠以惊人的速度在里面奔跑。我很震惊。

  note: 多摩动物园（Tama Zoo）位于东京。
- type: paragraph
  original: 'Tagami:  Usually I look at picture books and nature programs on television.  When I heard about Mr. Oomura’s trip to the aquarium, I also went to the zoo to observe the snakes.  But I watched the snakes for about 30 minutes, and they didn’t move at all. (laughs)'
  translation: 田上：通常我会看图画书和电视上的自然节目。当我听说大村先生去了水族馆，我也去了动物园观察蛇。但我看了大约30分钟，它们一动也不动。（笑）
  speaker: 田上玲子
- type: paragraph
  original: 'Ibe: I also went to zoos and pet shops where I could actually touch the animals.  I checked where the joints met and watched how the birds folded their wings. (laughs)'
  translation: 伊部：我也去了动物园和宠物店，在那里我可以实际触摸动物。我检查了关节的连接处，观察了鸟类如何折叠翅膀。（笑）
  speaker: 井部真那
  note: 伊部（Ibe）是设计师。

  translation: 大村：当然是实际观察活的动物。随着游戏开发变得繁忙，我们无法外出考察，但我们已经了解了动物的移动方式、捕食方式等，这让绘画变得更容易。

  original: 'Sugimori:  It’s as they said.  Many different things went into the Pokemon.  For example, with the Fire-type Pokemon, the pipes from furnaces and ironworks went into the shape of the body and the markings.  By tying different images together, new designs were born... [the rest of this interview will be translated and posted later].'
  translation: 杉森：正如他们所说。宝可梦融入了许多不同的元素。例如，对于火属性的宝可梦，熔炉和钢铁厂的管道融入了身体形状和花纹。通过将不同的意象联系在一起，新的设计诞生了……[本次访谈的其余部分将在稍后翻译并发布]。
