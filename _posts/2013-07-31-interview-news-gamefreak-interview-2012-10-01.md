- type: paragraph
  original: 'Junichi Masuda and Takao Unno have been deeply involved

    with the development of Pokémon video games for many years. Their understanding

    of the Pokémon universe and what goes into making a Pokémon game is unrivaled.

    Hear what they have to say about the new Pokémon games and much more.'
  translation: 增田顺一和海野隆雄多年来一直深入参与宝可梦电子游戏的开发。他们对宝可梦世界的理解以及对制作宝可梦游戏所需要素的把握是无与伦比的。来听听他们对新宝可梦游戏以及其他更多话题的看法吧。
  note: 增田顺一（Junichi Masuda）是 Game Freak 的董事，宝可梦系列的主要作曲家兼游戏总监；海野隆雄（Takao Unno）是 Game Freak 的设计师，曾参与多款宝可梦游戏的开发。
  speaker: 增田顺一
- type: paragraph
  original: 'P.Com: Thanks, Mr.

    Masuda and Mr. Unno, for spending some time with us. Give us a brief

    explanation of your jobs and what you do on a daily basis.'
  translation: P.Com：感谢增田先生和海野先生抽出时间接受我们的采访。请简要介绍一下你们的工作以及日常工作内容。
  speaker: Pokemon.com
- type: paragraph
  original: 'Mr. Masuda: That’s difficult! Well, I’m the development manager at GAME FREAK. I’m involved

    in the management of the company. But on a smaller level, I work as a

    game director, I’m a music composer, and I develop a lot of the

    scenarios of the game and more detailed work. I’m not sitting at my desk very

    often—I’m usually running around the office or I’m participating in meetings! A

    lot of different things.'
  translation: 增田先生：这个问题有点难回答！嗯，我是GAME FREAK的开发经理，参与公司的管理工作。但在更具体的层面上，我担任游戏总监，也是音乐作曲家，还负责游戏剧本的编写以及许多更细致的工作。我很少坐在办公桌前，通常是在办公室里跑来跑去，或者参加会议！要做的事情很多。
  speaker: 增田顺一
- type: paragraph
  original: 'Mr. Unno: As

    an art director, which is what I normally do, except for Pokémon Black Version 2 and Pokémon

    White Version 2 where I am the director, I’m involved in the general design

    direction for how to create Pokémon. I’m also involved in creating the 2-D and

    3-D graphics, as well as the user interface for the games. I also design

    Pokémon myself.'
  translation: 海野先生：我通常担任艺术总监，负责宝可梦的总体设计方向。同时我也参与2D和3D图形以及游戏用户界面的制作。我自己也会设计宝可梦。不过，在《宝可梦 黑2》和《宝可梦 白2》中，我担任的是总监一职。
  speaker: 海野隆雄
- type: paragraph
  original: 'As the overall director of Pokémon Black Version 2 and Pokémon

    White Version 2 , I was more involved in the general design of the games and

    bringing everyone together to develop them.'
  translation: 作为《宝可梦 黑2》和《宝可梦 白2》的总监，我更多地参与了游戏的整体设计，并协调大家共同开发。
  speaker: 海野隆雄
- type: paragraph
  original: 'P.Com: I’ve played

    a little bit of Pokémon Black Version 2 ,

    and it’s surprising how different these games feel from the outset than the

    first two games in Unova, from the look to the Pokémon you’ll encounter. They

    feel more original than, say, Pokémon

    Emerald does from Pokémon Ruby and Sapphire . Was this a particular

    point of emphasis during development?'
  translation: P.Com：我玩了一点《宝可梦 黑2》，令人惊讶的是，这两款游戏从一开始就与合众地区的前两款游戏感觉截然不同，无论是外观还是遇到的宝可梦。它们比《宝可梦 绿宝石》相对于《宝可梦 红宝石/蓝宝石》的感觉更具原创性。这是开发过程中的一个特别重点吗？
  speaker: Pokemon.com
  note: 这里提到的“前两款游戏”指的是《宝可梦 黑》和《宝可梦 白》。
- type: paragraph
  original: 'Mr. Unno: It

    was definitely something we paid attention to during development. When we first

    decided to make a true sequel to Pokémon

    Black Version and Pokémon White

    Version and set it two years later, we had two big concerns.'
  translation: 海野先生：这确实是我们在开发过程中关注的重点。当我们最初决定制作《宝可梦 黑》和《宝可梦 白》的真正续作，并将时间设定在两年后时，我们有两个主要的担忧。
  speaker: 海野隆雄
- type: paragraph
  original: 'One, by setting them in the same location it would kind

    of feel the same to the players who had played the previous games. The other

    was that as a sequel, it would maybe feel intimidating to new players, and they

    wouldn’t know what to do. We tried to get around that by giving the

    game an all-new story, increasing the number of Pokémon you can

    encounter in the region, introducing new towns, and making the protagonist a

    completely different character.'
  translation: 其一，如果设定在相同的地点，对于玩过前作的玩家来说可能会感觉雷同。其二，作为续作，可能会让新玩家感到不知所措，不知道该怎么玩。我们试图通过以下方式来解决这些问题：为游戏设计全新的故事，增加在该地区可遇到的宝可梦数量，引入新的城镇，并让主角成为一个完全不同的角色。
  speaker: 海野隆雄
- type: paragraph
  original: 'Plus, the route through the Unova region is also

    different. With all that, we think we have created a sense of freshness for

    both new and old players alike. Pretty much anyone can get in and enjoy the

    games. We also hope that new players may even become interested in the originals— Pokémon Black Version and Pokémon White Version —after enjoying Pokémon Black Version 2 or Pokémon White Version 2 .'
  translation: 此外，穿越合众地区的路线也有所不同。通过这些，我们认为我们为新老玩家都创造了新鲜感。几乎任何人都可以轻松上手并享受游戏。我们也希望新玩家在享受《宝可梦 黑2》或《宝可梦 白2》之后，甚至会对原作《宝可梦 黑》和《宝可梦 白》产生兴趣。
  speaker: 海野隆雄
- type: paragraph
  original: 'P.Com: How far

    into the development of the first games were you when you started working on

    the sequels ?'
  translation: P.Com：你们是在初代游戏开发到什么阶段时开始制作续作的？
  speaker: Pokemon.com
- type: paragraph
  original: 'Mr. Masuda: In terms of Pokémon Black Version 2 and Pokémon White Version 2 , it was

    actually near the end of the development of the first games when we started

    talking about what we wanted to do next. It was maybe only three or four months

    before the actual release of Pokémon

    Black Version and Pokémon White

    Version in Japan.'
  translation: 增田先生：就《宝可梦 黑2》和《宝可梦 白2》而言，实际上是在初代游戏开发接近尾声时，我们才开始讨论下一步想做什么。那时距离《宝可梦 黑》和《宝可梦 白》在日本正式发售可能只有三四个月的时间。
  speaker: 增田顺一
- type: paragraph
  original: 'By that time our internal staff was able to play the game

    even though it hadn’t been released yet. They were able to go through the whole

    scenario and start getting feedback about how the story was good, and they

    enjoyed characters such as N and Ghetsis. But our staff was very interested in

    what happened to these characters after the ending.'
  translation: 那时，我们的内部员工已经能够玩到这款游戏，尽管它还未发售。他们能够体验完整的剧情，并开始反馈故事如何精彩，他们喜欢N和魁奇思等角色。但我们的员工对这些角色在结局之后的故事非常感兴趣。
  speaker: 增田顺一
  note: N和魁奇思是《宝可梦 黑/白》中的主要角色。
- type: paragraph
  original: 'From all this feedback, we got the idea that would could

    expand on the story using those characters, and that’s when the idea of a

    direct sequel came up. We figured it’d also be a good challenge for us because

    we had never done a direct sequel before.'
  translation: 从这些反馈中，我们萌生了利用这些角色扩展故事的想法，就在这时，直接续作的点子出现了。我们认为这对我们来说也是一个很好的挑战，因为我们以前从未制作过直接续作。
  speaker: 增田顺一
- type: paragraph
  original: 'P.Com: Let’s talk

    about one new exciting feature, Pokéstar Studios. I’ve played it a little bit

    and it’s quite different from other experiences I’ve had in a Pokémon game.

    What was the inspiration for creating this feature?'
  translation: P.Com：让我们来谈谈一个令人兴奋的新功能——宝可梦好莱坞。我玩了一点，感觉与我在宝可梦游戏中的其他体验截然不同。创造这个功能的灵感是什么？
  speaker: Pokemon.com
- type: paragraph
  original: 'Mr. Unno: The

    main idea behind Pokéstar Studios was to really expand on the expressive visual

    opportunities of the Pokémon games. Whenever you’re making the game you have to

    keep track of the reality of the universe and make sure that everything is

    believable and isn’t something that’s too crazy. Creating a believable reality

    for the Pokémon universe is very important.'
  translation: 海野先生：宝可梦好莱坞背后的主要想法是真正扩展宝可梦游戏的表现性视觉机会。在制作游戏时，你必须保持宝可梦世界的现实感，确保一切可信，不会太过疯狂。为宝可梦世界创造一个可信的现实非常重要。
  speaker: 海野隆雄
- type: paragraph
  original: 'But, by making movies with your Pokémon and having them

    star in movies, you can put them in exciting scenarios that aren’t really

    possible in the reality of the Pokémon universe, such as battling against a

    giant UFO, traveling with a magnificent wizard, or battling a giant robot.

    Having these adventures should be very new and exciting for players.'
  translation: 但是，通过让你的宝可梦出演电影，你可以将它们置于宝可梦世界现实中不可能出现的激动人心的场景中，例如与巨大的UFO战斗、与一位伟大的巫师同行，或者与巨型机器人战斗。这些冒险对玩家来说应该是非常新奇和令人兴奋的。
  speaker: 海野隆雄
- type: paragraph
