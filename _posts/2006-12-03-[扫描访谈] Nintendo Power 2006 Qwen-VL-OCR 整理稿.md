      NP: Pokémon Diamond and Pokémon Pearl have the chance to introduce a big wave of new gamers to the Pokémon world. Have you made adjustments to your design approach with that in mind?
      Junichi Masuda: I definitely thought about how this game could bring in new people to the Pokémon world. The part that we really thought about thoroughly was to make sure that the people who are watching others play the game would want to join in and play the game themselves. For example, the touch-screen interface during battles—that was designed so that people would be lured in, or be attracted to the game, by watching players who were controlling the game with their hands and stylus. We wanted to make it something that people sitting next to the player would become curious about, so they would ask that player if they could try it out. We wanted this title to be something like when you open a toy box and turn it upside down, all these things would pour out of it where people would pick out what they like from the pile, saying "this looks fun!" So in that way, there's a whole lot of fun inside Diamond and Pearl.
    translation: |-
      NP：《宝可梦 钻石／珍珠》有机会为宝可梦世界带来一大批新玩家。你们在设计方法上是否为此做出了调整？
      增田顺一：我确实考虑过这款游戏如何能吸引新玩家进入宝可梦世界。我们真正深思熟虑的部分，是确保那些观看他人玩游戏的人会想要加入并亲自游玩。例如，战斗中的触屏界面——它的设计就是为了让旁观者看到玩家用手和触控笔操作时，会被吸引或对游戏产生兴趣。我们希望让坐在玩家旁边的人产生好奇心，从而问玩家他们是否可以试试看。我们希望这款作品就像打开一个玩具盒然后把它倒过来，所有东西都会倾泻而出，人们可以从这堆东西里挑出自己喜欢的，说“这个看起来很有趣！”。所以从这个意义上说，《钻石／珍珠》里充满了乐趣。

    original: |-
      NP: Do you consider Diamond and Pearl to be more or less difficult than previous games? How come?
      Masuda: When we made FireRed and LeafGreen, we intentionally made the game so that it's easier to play. But from that experience, I learned that if you make the game too easy, you need to explain a lot more about the game, making the game experience feel more complicated. So this time, we made it so that it would be easier for those people who are having a hard time figuring the game out and need a little help. As a result, I think Diamond and Pearl ended up being easier than Ruby and Sapphire, but is a little more difficult than FireRed and LeafGreen. Diamond and Pearl ended up being easier in the first half, making it easier for the players to get into gameplay, and it gets harder toward the later half of the game.
    translation: |-
      NP：你们认为《钻石／珍珠》比前作更难还是更简单？为什么？
      增田：在制作《火红／叶绿》时，我们有意让游戏更容易上手。但从那次经验中，我学到如果游戏做得太简单，就需要解释更多内容，反而让游戏体验感觉更复杂。所以这次，我们让那些在理解游戏方面有困难、需要一点帮助的玩家更容易上手。结果，我认为《钻石／珍珠》最终比《红宝石／蓝宝石》简单，但比《火红／叶绿》稍难一些。《钻石／珍珠》的前半部分更容易，让玩家更容易进入游戏，而后半部分难度会逐渐增加。
