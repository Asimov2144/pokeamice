- speaker: Takeshi Kawachimaru
  role: answer
  chapter: 相对论与反物质
  original: "I was the one that set the direction for Platinum. Mr. Masuda came to me and explained the original concept for the Pokemon Giratina. He mentioned anti-matter as something -- it's there, but it's something you can't see. Mr. Masuda gave me a lot of key concepts to design around -- matter, antimatter, Einstein's theory of relativity. I was the one that had to make those concepts a realization in a Pokemon game -- both visually and implementing certain tricks into the gameplay to give the player a sense of bipolarity."
  translation: 我是为《白金》确立整体创作方向的总监。当时增田先生找到我，向我解释了关于传说的宝可梦‘骑拉帝纳’（Giratina）的原始构想。他特别提到了‘反物质’（Antimatter）的概念——它确实客观存在，但人类却无法用肉眼直接观测。增田先生赋予了我一系列核心设计支柱：物质、反物质、以及爱因斯坦的相对论。而我的使命，就是必须在《宝可梦》的视听与玩法机制中将这些高度抽象的理论具象化，让玩家在探索中直观感知到两极对立的二元宇宙感。
- speaker: Takeshi Kawachimaru
  role: answer
  chapter: 相对论与反物质
  original: "There's a concept in particle physics called CP violation. It helps explain the relationship between matter and antimatter. The relationship is bipolar. The reality is that anti-matter is something that's very, very fragile. This concept weighs heavily in how we came up with Pokemon Platinum's Distortion World."
  translation: 在粒子物理学中，有一个著名的核心概念叫做‘CP对称性破缺’（CP violation），它解释了物质与反物质之间的非对称关系。这种关系呈现出本质上的两极对立，而客观事实是，反物质是一种极度脆弱、稍纵即逝的存在。正是物理学中的这一脆弱性与平衡规律，极大地启发了我们对于《白金》中‘破损世界’（Distortion World / やぶれたせかい）的构筑。

- speaker: Takeshi Kawachimaru
  role: answer
  chapter: 破损世界的营造
  original: "One of the things I, along with the other designers, wanted to focus on in Distortion World is creating a place that's very similar to Sinnoh, but very different. It's definitely a real world, but as soon as the player enters Distortion World, I want to make sure they know that it's different. For example, the sky is a little bit darker in Distortion World than it is in Sinnoh. The player will see lots of plants that they've never seen before. Waterfalls will have water flowing in strange directions. There are many similar elements between the real world and Distortion World -- whether it's movement, shape, or color -- but we want to make sure the player feels that they're in a distinct and opposite world."
  translation: 我与其他关卡设计师在破损世界中最聚焦的一点，就是创造一个‘与神奥极为相似、却又截然相反’的平行领域。它绝非虚幻的梦境，而是一个冰冷真实的异空间。但当玩家踏入的一瞬间，我们必须让他们立刻意识到周遭规律的彻底崩解。例如，破损世界的天空比神奥更加昏暗压抑；玩家会看到现实中从未见过的诡异植物；甚至连重力都发生紊乱，瀑布会违背常理反向奔流。无论动势、形态还是色调，它与现实既互为镜像倒影，又处处昭示着这是一个绝对相反的颠倒空间。

- speaker: Junichi Masuda
  role: answer
  chapter: 力量体系与阿尔宙斯
  original: "In Diamond and Pearl you have Dialga and Palkia, representing Time and Space. In Platinum you have Giratina, representing Antimatter and the other dimension. And Arceus was conceived as the origin of it all. When designing these ultimate beings, we look at the cosmic balance rather than simply making power numbers bigger. The mythology of Sinnoh represents the origin of the universe itself, but the charm of Pokemon will always be that even a small, beloved partner can stand beside these cosmic legends."
  translation: 在《钻石·珍珠》中，帝牙卢卡与帕路奇亚分别司掌‘时间’与‘空间’；而在《白金》中，骑拉帝纳则象征着‘反物质’与平行的异度维度。阿尔宙斯在设定中确实被塑造成这一切宇宙维度的诞生源点。然而在设计这些究极神话存在时，我们所考量的是宇宙运转的哲学平衡，而绝非盲目无休止地堆砌战力数值。神奥的神话描摹了宇宙本身的起源，但《宝可梦》恒久不变的魅力在于：即便是一只弱小却饱含深情的旅伴，也能与这些宏大的宇宙传说并肩而立。
