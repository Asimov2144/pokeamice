Don't get my wrong, I'm still excited and thrilled that I get to chat with people like Chris Taylor, Todd Hollenshead, etc., but, to use a wrestling term, I seldom "mark out" when I get to meet people such as these big players. Yesterday was one of those rare exceptions.
  translation: 虽然我很荣幸能见到一些世界上最出色的游戏开发者，但我报道游戏已经够久了，见到“著名”游戏设计师时很少会紧张。别误会，能和 Chris Taylor、Todd Hollenshead 这样的人聊天，我仍然很兴奋、很激动，但用摔角术语来说，见到这类大人物时我很少会“mark out”。昨天就是罕见的例外之一。
- original: You see, yesterday, I went to Nintendo's office to chat with GameFreak director Junicihi Masuda and game designer Takeshi Kawachimaru. Masuda-san has been with the company since its inception, working on the original Pokemon role-playing games. He's one of the big reasons I've lost hundreds (thousands?) of hours of my life to Pokemon. Kawachimaru has also been contributing to Pokemon games for a long time, as well getting the spotlight in Pokemon Platinum (due out next week!). I chatted with the two about Platinum, particle physics, the Pokemon phenomenon, the surprising complexity of the series, future Pokemon games, and Masuda-san's fascination with bridges. I also turned into a total fan, asking them to sign some of my Pokemon goods and pose with some of my Pokemon toys. Here's my interview with these distinguished gentlemen from GameFreak.
  translation: "事情是这样的，昨天我去了任天堂的办公室，和 GAME FREAK 的总监增田顺一以及游戏设计师河内丸武史聊天。增田先生从公司创立之初就在那里工作，参与制作了最初的宝可梦角色扮演游戏。我人生中在宝可梦上花掉的几百（几千？）个小时，很大程度上要归功于他。河内丸也长期参与宝可梦游戏的制作，并在《宝可梦 白金》（下周发售！）中获得了更多关注。我和两人聊了白金、粒子物理、宝可梦现象、系列令人惊讶的复杂性、未来的宝可梦游戏，以及增田先生对桥的痴迷。我还彻底变成了粉丝，请他们在我的宝可梦周边上签名，并和我的宝可梦玩具一起合影。以下就是我对这两位来自 GAME FREAK 的杰出绅士的采访。"
- original: Pokemon Platinum's concept is based on some heady things like Einstein's theory of relativity, matter, and antimatter. Would you kindly expand on that for the G4 audience?
  role: question
  speaker: G4
  translation: 《宝可梦 白金》的概念基于一些很深奥的东西，比如爱因斯坦的相对论、物质和反物质。能请你们为 G4 的读者展开讲讲吗？
- original: I was the one that set the direction for Platinum. Mr. Masuda came to me and explained the original concept for the Pokemon Giratina. He mentioned anti-matter as something -- it's there, but it's something you can't see. Mr. Masuda gave me a lot of key concepts to design around -- matter, antimatter, Einstein's theory of relativity. I was the one that had to make those concepts a realization in a Pokemon game -- both visually and implementing certain tricks into the gameplay to give the player a sense of bipolarity.
  role: answer
  speaker: 河内丸武史
  translation: 为《白金》定下方向的人是我。增田先生来找我，解释了宝可梦骑拉帝纳的原始概念。他提到反物质是一种——它存在，但你看不见的东西。增田先生给了我很多用于设计的关键概念——物质、反物质、爱因斯坦的相对论。我必须把这些概念在宝可梦游戏中实现出来——既要在视觉上实现，也要在玩法中加入一些技巧，让玩家感受到一种两极对立。
- original: There's a concept in particle, in physics called CP violation. It helps explain the relationship between matter and antimatter. The relationship is bipolar. The reality is that anti-matter is something that's very, very fragile. This concept weighs heavily in how we came up with Pokemon Platinum's Distortion World.
  role: answer
  speaker: 河内丸武史
  translation: 粒子物理中有一个概念叫 CP 破坏。它有助于解释物质和反物质之间的关系。这种关系是两极对立的。现实是，反物质是一种非常非常脆弱的东西。这个概念在很大程度上影响了我们构思《宝可梦 白金》的毁坏的世界的方式。
- original: How are some of these concepts realized? What will gamers see and feel when they're in Distortion World?
  role: question
  speaker: G4
  translation: 这些概念是如何实现的？玩家在毁坏的世界中会看到什么、感受到什么？
- original: One of the things I, along with the other designers, wanted to focus on in Distortion World is creating a place that's very similar to Sinnoh [the world of Pokemon Diamond/Pearl], but very different. It's definitely a real world, but as soon as the player enters Distortion World, I want to make sure they know that it's different. For example, the sky is a little bit darker in Distortion World than it is in Sinnoh. The player will see lots of plants that they've never seen before. Waterfalls will have water flowing in strange directions. There are many similar elements between the real world and Distortion World -- whether it's movement, shape, or color -- but we want to make sure the player feels that they're in a distinct and opposite world.
  role: answer
  speaker: 河内丸武史
  translation: 我和其他设计师在毁坏的世界中想要着重做的一件事，是创造一个与神奥地区（《宝可梦 钻石／珍珠》的世界）非常相似、但又非常不同的地方。它绝对是一个真实的世界，但玩家一进入毁坏的世界，我就想让他们知道这里不一样。比如，毁坏的世界的天空比神奥地区稍暗一些。玩家会看到许多从未见过的植物。瀑布的水会以奇怪的方向流动。真实世界和毁坏的世界之间有许多相似的元素——无论是运动、形状还是颜色——但我们想确保玩家感到自己身处一个截然不同、彼此对立的世界。
- original: Along with millions of competitive players, I'm personally looking forward to Battle Frontier. Would you tell G4 readers what they can expect in Battle Frontier?
  role: question
  speaker: G4
  translation: 和数百万对战玩家一样，我个人也很期待对战开拓区。你能告诉 G4 的读者，对战开拓区里有什么可期待的吗？
- original: The kind of battling you do in Battle Frontier is very different from the battling you do throughout the game's storyline. There are different rules. You'll want to use different strategies. You'll want to raise Pokemon differently. You'll need extensive knowledge of Pokemon and their moves to confront the trainers in Battle Frontier. When you play the storyline, you pretty much play alone and complete the game. In Battle Frontier, you work with friends, siblings, parents, or children. You can play together. We wanted to create an opportunity for communication, so that families and friends can talk about the different strategies they can employ.
  role: answer
  speaker: 河内丸武史
  translation: 你在对战开拓区里进行的对战，和你在整个游戏剧情中进行的对战非常不同。规则不同。你会想使用不同的策略。你会想用不同的方式培养宝可梦。你需要对宝可梦及其招式有广泛了解，才能面对对战开拓区里的训练家。玩剧情时，你基本上是独自游玩并通关。而在对战开拓区，你和朋友、兄弟姐妹、父母或孩子一起合作。你们可以一起玩。我们想创造一个交流的机会，让家人和朋友可以讨论他们能采用的不同策略。
- original: Legendary Pokemon have been getting more powerful since gamers encountered Articuno, Moltres, and Zapdos in Kanto. Giratina, the cover Pokemon for Platinum, is ridiculously powerful. Arceus is like the god of all Pokemon. Will there be anything more powerful than Arceus in future games?

- original: In Diamond and Pearl you have Dialga and Palkia. In Platinum you have Giratina. There are three legendary Pokemon and there's a relationship between the three. From there we have a story to move onto with other legendary Pokemon, but at this point that's all I can share.
  role: answer

- original: One of the core concepts of the Pokemon games is trading. You can trade within the game, but you can also go out and meet with your friends to trade. You can talk with your family and friends as you play Pokemon. For this concept, the most suitable hardware is portable, like the Nintendo DS. That's why we focus on portable game systems.
  role: answer

  role: question

- original: Our art director, Ken Sugimori and I spend a lot of time talking about Pokemon. Since Pikachu is very popular, we wanted to create a Pokemon that was as cute as Pikachu, but we were having a very difficult time. Pikachu is so popular that it was hard to come up with something just as appealing. So we talked a lot and decided to come up with a Pokemon that would eventually evolve into Pikachu. We spent a lot of time designing Pichu and that's why I have a special love for this Pokemon.
  role: answer
  speaker: 增田顺一
  translation: 我们的艺术总监杉森建和我花了很多时间讨论宝可梦。因为皮卡丘非常受欢迎，我们想创造一只和皮卡丘一样可爱的宝可梦，但当时非常困难。皮卡丘太受欢迎了，很难想出同样有吸引力的东西。所以我们讨论了很多，决定做一只最终会进化成皮卡丘的宝可梦。我们在设计皮丘上花了很多时间，这就是我对这只宝可梦有特殊感情的原因。
  note: 杉森建是Game Freak的创始成员之一，长期担任宝可梦系列的艺术总监和角色设计。

- original: Many people erroneously think that Pokemon games are just for children, but there are many complex features in the game like IVs, EV training, Hidden Power, and more. How did these features come about?
  role: question
  speaker: G4
  translation: 很多人误以为宝可梦游戏只是给小孩子玩的，但游戏里有很多复杂的机制，比如个体值、努力值训练、觉醒力量等等。这些机制是怎么来的？
- original: The reason why we put things like IVs and EVs into the game is because we wanted to give players that are really into Pokemon battles much more to work with and much more to enjoy. We have a team that's dedicated to constantly enhancing and evolving the battle system. They have so many ideas and such a willingness to enhance the game. They're always thinking of ways to make battles more interesting. One example from this generation is that they separated moves into physical attacks and special attacks, which adds more versatility and strategy to both offense and defense. They always listen to player feedback and they battle a lot themselves. This team is the reason why Pokemon has such a rich battle system that's always evolving.
  role: answer
  speaker: 增田顺一
