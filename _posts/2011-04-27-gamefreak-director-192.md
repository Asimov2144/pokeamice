AnnyongHaseyo!

On April 21st, Korean version of Pokemon Black and White

have launched.

I visited Korea for the launch event.

Korea is the country I always wanted to go!!

Leaving Haneda(Tokyo), and

arriving at Gimpo airport(Korea).

Heading to the center of the city by car.

<img src="/assets/images/gamefreak-director/archive/192/en/192a.jpg" alt="" loading="lazy">

On the way to the city,

there are many beautiful bridges.

<img src="/assets/images/gamefreak-director/archive/192/en/192b.jpg" alt="" loading="lazy">

Skyscrapers, same as Tokyo.

Speaking of Korea, delicious meals!

<img src="/assets/images/gamefreak-director/archive/192/en/192c.jpg" alt="" loading="lazy">

Let’s eat something. I ate live octopus!! It was moving…

<img src="/assets/images/gamefreak-director/archive/192/en/192d.jpg" alt="" loading="lazy">

I ate crabs, too. It was spicy, but somewhat tasty.

And Pokemon Korea office.

<img src="/assets/images/gamefreak-director/archive/192/en/192e.jpg" alt="" loading="lazy">

Many staffs were working!

Beautiful sight from the rooftop.

<img src="/assets/images/gamefreak-director/archive/192/en/192f.jpg" alt="" loading="lazy">

<img src="/assets/images/gamefreak-director/archive/192/en/192g.jpg" alt="" loading="lazy">

Little fire extinguisher got my attention.

<img src="/assets/images/gamefreak-director/archive/192/en/192h.jpg" alt="" loading="lazy">

Fat and cute. Bottlish…

Heading to the hotel.

<img src="/assets/images/gamefreak-director/archive/192/en/192i.jpg" alt="" loading="lazy">

View from the hotel was fantastic.

Cool skyscrapers.

And the morning of launch day, 21st.

<img src="/assets/images/gamefreak-director/archive/192/en/192j.jpg" alt="" loading="lazy">

Time is nine o’clock in the morning.

Some people start waiting from 8:30 in the morning.

It was cold morning, but thank you for coming!!

Many people visited to this event!!

<img src="/assets/images/gamefreak-director/archive/192/en/oma.jpg" alt="" loading="lazy">

I’m just so happy!!!!

<img src="/assets/images/gamefreak-director/archive/192/en/192k.jpg" alt="" loading="lazy">

Life size dolls of first three Pokemon!!

<img src="/assets/images/gamefreak-director/archive/192/en/192l.jpg" alt="" loading="lazy">

First, self introduction.

<img src="/assets/images/gamefreak-director/archive/192/en/omb.jpg" alt="" loading="lazy">

Ta-da—!

<img src="/assets/images/gamefreak-director/archive/192/en/192m.jpg" alt="" loading="lazy">

Reshiram, Zekrom and the chair I used for the signing autographs.

<img src="/assets/images/gamefreak-director/archive/192/en/omc.jpg" alt="" loading="lazy">

Signing autographs.

<img src="/assets/images/gamefreak-director/archive/192/en/192n.jpg" alt="" loading="lazy">

Hangul is coordinated by vowels and consonants.

If you learn Hangul, you soon be able to learn it.

This is my first time to write in Hangul, the name of girl fan.

All of Korea Pokemon fans are nice and warm.

Thank you very much for the letter and drawings.

I’ve received a lot of energy from many people.

I’ll work as hard as I could.

After the event, I visited Myungdong and found a banner.

<img src="/assets/images/gamefreak-director/archive/192/en/192o.jpg" alt="" loading="lazy">

It says “People in Japan, please don’t give up. Hang on.

We are friend of yours.”

I’m extremely moved.

People of Korea, Thank you very much!

KamsaHamnida!

Ciao!
