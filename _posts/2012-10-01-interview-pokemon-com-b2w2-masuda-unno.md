- original: 'P.Com: I’ve played a little bit of Pokémon Black Version 2, and it’s surprising how different these games feel from the outset than the first two games in Unova, from the look to the Pokémon you’ll encounter.   speaker: Pokemon.com

- original: It was definitely something we paid attention to during development. When we first decided to make a true sequel to Pokémon Black Version and Pokémon White Version and set it two years later, we had two big concerns.
  role: answer
  speaker: 海野隆雄
  translation: 这绝对是我们在开发过程中关注的事情。当我们最初决定制作《宝可梦 黑版》和《宝可梦 白版》的真正续作，并将时间设定在两年后时，我们有两个大的顾虑。
- original: One, by setting them in the same location it would kind of feel the same to the players who had played the previous games. The other was that as a sequel, it would maybe feel intimidating to new players, and they wouldn’t know what to do. We tried to get around that by giving the game an all-new story, increasing the number of Pokémon you can encounter in the region, introducing new towns, and making the protagonist a completely different character.

  speaker: 海野隆雄

- original: Plus, the route through the Unova region is also different. With all that, we think we have created a sense of freshness for both new and old players alike. Pretty much anyone can get in and enjoy the games. We also hope that new players may even become interested in the originals—Pokémon Black Version and Pokémon White Version—after enjoying Pokémon Black Version 2 or Pokémon White Version 2.

  speaker: 海野隆雄
  translation: 此外，穿越合众地区的路线也不同了。有了这些，我们认为我们为新老玩家都创造了新鲜感。几乎任何人都能上手并享受这些游戏。我们也希望新玩家在享受《宝可梦 黑版2》或《宝可梦 白版2》之后，甚至可能对原作——《宝可梦 黑版》和《宝可梦 白版》——产生兴趣。

- original: The main idea behind Pokéstar Studios was to really expand on the expressive visual opportunities of the Pokémon games. Whenever you’re making the game you have to keep track of the reality of the universe and make sure that everything is believable and isn’t something that’s too crazy. Creating a believable reality for the Pokémon universe is very important.

- original: But, by making movies with your Pokémon and having them star in movies, you can put them in exciting scenarios that aren’t really possible in the reality of the Pokémon universe, such as battling against a giant UFO, traveling with a magnificent wizard, or battling a giant robot.   speaker: 海野隆雄
  translation: 宝可梦好莱坞另一个令人兴奋的地方是，你要按照剧本来，但战斗使用的是实际游戏机制。而通过遵循剧本，你能从拍摄这些电影中学到很多宝可梦对战的基本要素。

  role: question
  speaker: Pokemon.com
  translation: P.Com：一直以来，似乎都很强调在玩家与游戏角色之间建立强烈的个人羁绊。您为什么觉得这在宝可梦游戏中很重要？
- original: As you know, the protagonist in the game never speaks. The idea is to make it feel like the player is that character as much as possible. By doing this we’re also hoping that the player has a better bond with the Pokémon they encounter, as well as the people they meet inside the game.
  role: answer
  speaker: 海野隆雄
  translation: 如您所知，游戏中的主角从不说话。这样设计是为了让玩家尽可能感觉自己是那个角色。通过这样做，我们也希望玩家能更好地与他们遇到的宝可梦，以及在游戏内遇到的人建立羁绊。

- original: The idea of the Memory Link was to allow players who had played the first games to enjoy the story on perhaps a deeper level, with the chance to see how the Unova region has changed, as well as how the people—their ideas and feelings—have changed. The Memory Link is really sort of a “present” to the players of the first games.
  role: answer
  speaker: 海野隆雄
  translation: 记忆链接的想法是让玩过第一作的玩家能在或许更深的层次上享受故事，有机会看到合众地区如何变化，以及人们——他们的想法和感受——如何变化。记忆链接其实是给第一作玩家的一份“礼物”。
- original: 'P.Com: What was behind the decision to create two follow-up games as opposed to a single game, as Pokémon Platinum was to Pokémon Diamond and Pokémon Pearl?'
  role: question
  speaker: Pokemon.com
  translation: P.Com：决定制作两款后续作品而不是像《宝可梦 白金》之于《宝可梦 钻石／珍珠》那样只做一款，背后的原因是什么？

  speaker: 增田顺一
  translation: 另外，由于这些游戏是真正的续作，感觉从前两作发展过来要自然得多。
- original: 'P.Com: In the 15 years that Pokémon games have been around, they have been remarkably consistent in their style and quality. Do you have a philosophy for how you produce games over the years?'
  role: question
  speaker: Pokemon.com
  translation: P.Com：在宝可梦游戏走过的15年里，它们在风格和品质上一直非常稳定。多年来，您对如何制作游戏有什么理念吗？
- original: That’s a difficult question. Obviously our focus is always on quality. Whenever we make a game, we concentrate on what first-time players will think when they play it. Those first-time players, they change as time goes on. Nowadays kids have computers and the Internet. That kind of technology is commonplace, and sort of a given for them, where that wasn’t really the case before. So we’re always focusing on that new audience.
  role: answer
  speaker: 增田顺一
  translation: 这是个很难回答的问题。显然我们始终注重品质。每当制作游戏时，我们都会专注于初次游玩的玩家在玩的时候会怎么想。这些初次游玩的玩家会随着时间而变化。如今孩子们有电脑和互联网。这类技术对他们来说司空见惯，几乎是理所当然的，而以前并非如此。所以我们始终关注新的受众。
- original: Also, in regard to graphics and animation, there’s sort of a universal sense of what’s comfortable... what’s natural to everyone across the world. We want to make the game feel comfortable for everyone in the world, no matter where they live. We make sure that everyone in the staff is aware of this and that we’re all on the same page—that’s important to the design.
  role: answer
  speaker: 增田顺一
  translation: 另外，在画面和动画方面，世界上存在着一种普遍意义上的舒适感……对全世界所有人来说都很自然的东西。我们希望让游戏对世界上任何人来说都感到舒适，无论他们住在哪里。我们确保所有员工都意识到这一点，并且大家达成共识——这对设计来说很重要。

  role: answer
  speaker: 增田顺一
  translation: 我们当然以前也想过这些事。比如，改变故事中道馆的数量，或者让初始宝可梦的选择变成五只而不是三只，或者让初始宝可梦有钢属性或毒属性这样的不同属性。

  role: answer
  speaker: 增田顺一
  translation: 但每次做新游戏时，我们都会尝试从零开始，思考怎样才能做出一款真正优秀的游戏。每当我们以这种方式重新构建时，往往会发现最合理的、最容易理解的还是同样的那些要素。
- original: For example, having three starter Pokémon of three types that work in a kind of rock-paper-scissors relationship is very easy to understand for players. So, very often—even though we’re rebuilding it every time—the games end up having similar construction.
  role: answer
  speaker: 增田顺一
  translation: 比如，三只不同属性的初始宝可梦形成一种类似石头剪刀布的关系，这对玩家来说非常容易理解。所以，尽管我们每次都在重新构建，游戏最终往往还是会有相似的结构。
- original: 'P.Com: Many of the Pokémon are obviously inspired by real-world animals and objects. Mr. Masuda, I know that you travel quite a bit. Can you say that there are places you’ve been that directly inspire locations in the game in the same way as Pokémon themselves?'
  role: question

In more general terms, I try to be inspired by everything, by noticing and observing differences in the places I go.   speaker: 增田顺一

- original: 'P.Com: There are so many Pokémon now, as well as so many different ways to battle, such as Triple Battles and Rotation Battles, the testing to ensure gameplay balance must be incredibly difficult.   role: question
  speaker: Pokemon.com
