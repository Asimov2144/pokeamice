  original: "What was the reasoning behind doing a sequel to Black and White opposed to doing something like Pokémon Grey?"

- speaker: Junichi Masuda
  role: answer
  chapter: 企划颠覆与拒绝妥协
  original: "A lot of people probably expected us to follow the same pattern we've always followed and come out with 'Pokémon Grey.' But whenever everyone thinks they know what we're going to do next, we always want to surprise them! Near the end of Black and White's development, the team was having so much fun with the Unova region that we started talking: 'What will happen to these characters two years later?'"
  translation: 【制作人 增田顺一】：全世界绝大多数玩家想必都理所当然地以为，我们会按部就班地沿着过去的轨迹推出一部《宝可梦 灰》。然而，每当所有人都自以为猜中我们下一步要做什么的时候，我们骨子里最强烈的冲动，就是一定要打破所有人的预期、带来前所未有的巨大惊喜！在《黑·白》即将完工的阶段，全体研发团队都在合众地方的广袤世界里玩得不亦乐乎，大家开始热烈讨论：“两年之后，这片大地上的人们和宝可梦到底会经历怎样的命运剧变呢？”
- speaker: Junichi Masuda
  role: answer
  chapter: 企划颠覆与拒绝妥协
  original: "Furthermore, the core philosophical theme of Black and White was dualism -- two opposing concepts of Truth and Ideals. If we merged them together into 'Grey,' we would be diluting that entire philosophical foundation. Black and White needed to stay distinct, and the only honest way to continue that story was to make Black 2 and White 2."
  translation: 【增田顺一】：更深层的原因在于，本作最底层的哲学基石是严密的二元论——‘真实’与‘理想’是两极对立、永恒激辩的主张。如果我们草率地将它们混杂中和成一个所谓的‘灰色’，就等于把前作耗尽心血构筑的全部哲学骨骼彻底稀释击碎了！黑与白必须始终各自傲然挺立，而延续这份波澜壮阔史诗的唯一忠实途径，就是堂堂正正打造两部正统续作——《黑2》与《白2》。

  original: "Why keep the game on the Nintendo DS instead of moving to the Nintendo 3DS, which was already out?"
  translation: 既然当时任天堂3DS已经正式上市，为什么依然坚持将《黑2·白2》留在NDS平台呢？
- speaker: Junichi Masuda
  role: answer
  chapter: 硬件平台与联机生态
  original: "First, being a direct sequel to Black and White, we really wanted players who had already enjoyed Black and White to be able to jump right into the sequel on the exact same hardware they already owned. Second, Pokémon is fundamentally a communication game. There were already over 150 million Nintendo DS systems out there in players' hands worldwide. We wanted to make sure that as many players as possible could trade, battle, and experience the new 100-player Fes Missions together without any barriers."
  translation: 【增田顺一】：首先，作为《黑·白》血脉相连的正统续作，我们最强烈的期盼就是让那些已经沉浸其中的数千万玩家，无需追加任何新的硬件负担，就能直接在自己最熟悉的主机上无缝开启崭新的后传篇章。其次，《宝可梦》本质上是一款关于‘人与人之间交流连接’的社交游戏。当时全球已经有超过1.5亿台NDS主机握在不同国家玩家的手中。我们下定决心，必须确保每一个普通的孩子和老玩家，都能在没有任何经济门槛的阻隔下，跨越国界自由交换对战、共同畅享百人同屏的盛大祭典任务！
