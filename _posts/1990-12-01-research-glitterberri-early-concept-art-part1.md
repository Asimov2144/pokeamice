- original: In 1989, two events happened that were important for the development of Pokémon. First off, Nintendo released the Game Boy. 5 days later, future Pokémon developers Satoshi Tajiri and Ken Sugimori turned Game Freak, their self-published gaming fanzine, into a video game company.
  translation: 1989年，发生了两件对宝可梦开发至关重要的事件。首先，任天堂发布了Game Boy。五天后，未来的宝可梦开发者田尻智和杉森建将他们自行出版的游戏同人志GAME FREAK转变为一家电子游戏公司。

- original: The Game Boy was originally seen as a system geared towards puzzle and action games. Indeed, the handheld saw many releases that fell into these categories. Then came the release of a Square RPG known in English as The Final Fantasy Legend. When Tajiri saw how successful it was, he realized that even non-action games were possible on a portable system.
  translation: Game Boy最初被视为一款面向益智和动作游戏的系统。事实上，这款掌机上确实发布了许多属于这些类别的游戏。随后，一款在英文中被称为《魔界塔士 沙加》的史克威尔RPG问世。当田尻智看到它如此成功时，他意识到即使是非动作游戏在便携系统上也是可能的。

- original: What Tajiri really had his eye on, however, was the Game Boy’s linking functionality. This data transfer system inspired the developer to dream up a game in which players would be able to trade with each other. Tajiri was also a fan of Ultra Seven, a TV fantasy series that was a followup to Ultraman, from which he got the idea of capsule monsters. He adopted the name Capsule Monsters for the RPG he set out to plan. The game, he imagined, would have monsters in containers similar to capsule toys travelling back and forth via the Game Link Cable. In the fall of 1990, Tajiri brought the project proposal he’d created to Nintendo, who decided to fund its development.
  translation: 然而，田尻智真正看中的是Game Boy的联机功能。这种数据传输系统激发了这位开发者的梦想，他构想出一款玩家能够互相交换的游戏。田尻智也是《赛文奥特曼》的粉丝，这部电视奇幻剧是《奥特曼》的续作，他从其中获得了胶囊怪兽的灵感。他为自己计划中的RPG采用了“胶囊怪兽”（Capsule Monsters）这个名字。他想象中，游戏里的怪兽会像胶囊玩具一样装在容器中，通过Game Link Cable来回传输。1990年秋天，田尻智将他创作的项目企划书带到了任天堂，任天堂决定资助其开发。

- original: Tajiri was unable to trademark the name Capsule Monsters due to copyright issues, and the shortened version of the game’s name, Capumon, just didn’t sound very nice. Accordingly, he revised the title to Pocket Monsters, which was then shortened to “Pokémon”.
  translation: 由于版权问题，田尻智无法为“胶囊怪兽”这个名字注册商标，而游戏名称的缩写“Capumon”听起来也不太好。因此，他将标题改为“Pocket Monsters”，后来缩写为“Pokémon”。
