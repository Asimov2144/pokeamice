- speaker: Pokemon.com
  role: question
  chapter: 十年变迁与团队心境
  original: "It has been ten years since the original Pokémon Gold and Silver versions. What has changed in that time with regards to your approach to game design and what has remained the same?"
  translation: 距离最初的《宝可梦 金／银》发售已经过去了整整十年。在这十年光阴中，你们在游戏设计理念上有哪些翻天覆地的变化？又有哪些核心信念自始至终未曾改变？
- speaker: Junichi Masuda, Director
  role: answer
  chapter: 十年变迁与团队心境
  original: "Because of the hardware, our game design has changed dramatically. Really, I didn’t think of the Internet ten years ago! (laughs) Also, the quality of both picture and sound have dramatically advanced in that time—the number of colors and three-dimensional graphics, for instance, has greatly expanded. Of course, that made things harder! (laughs) One thing which hasn’t changed is our desire to create a game that is approachable for everyone."
  translation: 【制作人 增田顺一】：受惠于硬件平台的飞跃，我们的游戏设计发生了天翻地覆的变革。说真的，十年前做原版金银时，我脑子里压根连互联网的概念都没有呢！（笑）此外，画面与声音的质感在这期间实现了跨越式的进阶——色彩数与全3D立体场景的表现力无限延展。当然，这同时也让开发难度成倍激增！（笑）而唯一恒久不变的，是我们竭尽全力要打造一款‘任何人都能轻松融入、感受快乐’的游戏初心。
- speaker: Shigeki Morimoto, Game Director
  role: answer
  chapter: 十年变迁与团队心境
  original: "It’s also worth noting that what has changed was that more people are now involved in the development. As a result, there are a lot of things we can do, but now we need somebody who can lead and hold the team together. Our passion for game creation has remained the same."
  translation: 【游戏总监 森本茂树】：另一个巨大的转变在于参与开发的团队人数规模大幅增加了。由此我们可以实现许多当年受限于人手而放弃的构想，但也更加需要有力的领导者将大家拧成一股绳。而我们对待游戏创作的那份炽热激情，十年来从未衰减半分。
- speaker: Shigeru Ohmori, Planner
  role: answer
  chapter: 十年变迁与团队心境
  original: "When Gold and Silver launched, I was a student, and I played the game intensely. I never thought I would be involved in Pokémon game development. For me personally, this has been a huge change. I still have vivid memories of playing those games, and it encourages me to do my best to make an even better game for future players."
  translation: 【策划总监 大森滋】：当年《金·银》初代上市时，我还只是一名普通的学生，每天通宵达旦、极度沉迷地玩着这款游戏。当时的我做梦也无法想象，十年后自己竟然能作为主创深度参与到这部经典神作的重制开发中。对我个人而言，这是生命中无与伦比的剧变。十年前握着Game Boy的那份感动与记忆至今历历在目，它无时无刻不在鞭策我：一定要倾尽全力，为新一代的玩家打造一部更加精彩的杰作！
- speaker: Akito Mori, Programming Lead
  role: answer
  chapter: 底层架构与美术克制
  original: "Most players will know that one thing that has changed is that the number of Pokémon has doubled— as a result, the data sizes have increased as well. This means we that need to make sure that one new idea is not going to affect any other Pokémon data. But one thing that has never changed is that we are a group of 'Game Freaks,' just like our company name says."
  translation: 【主程序员 森昭人】：广大玩家都明白，宝可梦的数量比起十年前整整翻了一倍，随之而来的是底层数据规模几何级数的膨胀。这意味着我们加入任何一个崭新的系统创意，都必须反复验证绝不能破坏其他几百只宝可梦的数据结构稳定性。但唯一不变的是，我们始终是一帮人如其名的‘游戏极客（Game Freaks）’。
- speaker: Takao Unno, Art Director
  role: answer
  chapter: 底层架构与美术克制
  original: "As we’ve noted, we are now able to express various aspects of the games differently thanks to the technological advancement of the hardware. However, our stance on precisely what new technology can be utilized hasn’t changed. We do not use all available technology—instead, we select those that are best for the players and those that make the gameplay more compelling."
  translation: 【艺术总监 海野隆雄】：正如大家所言，硬件算力的暴涨赋予了我们极其多样化的视觉表现手段。然而，我们对于‘如何审慎运用新技术’的克制立场从未动摇。我们绝不会盲目炫耀所有可用技术——相反，我们只精准甄选那些真正能造福玩家体验、让游戏机制更具沉浸与魅力的技术手段。

- speaker: Pokemon.com
  role: question
  chapter: 全球共鸣与计步器
  original: "It's unusual for a game to be very popular both in Japan and in the United States (and for that matter, in Europe). What makes Pokémon so universally enjoyed?"
  translation: 一款游戏能够同时在日本、北美乃至整个欧洲掀起旷日持久的风靡热潮，在游戏史上是极其罕见的。究竟是什么让《宝可梦》获得了如此具有普世性的全球热爱？
- speaker: Masuda
  role: answer
  chapter: 全球共鸣与计步器
  original: "First of all, it is not just a game series that simply presents the Pokémon world, characters, and scenarios. It is the entire gameplay experience that goes way beyond the borders. For instance, let’s take a universal play mechanic like throwing and catching a ball. Children from different countries can play that game right away. In Pokémon games, encountering a Pokémon, battling, catching, and trading are universal mechanics at the core."
  translation: 【增田顺一】：首先，宝可梦绝非仅仅向玩家单向展示某种奇幻世界观、角色或剧本，而是整套‘游戏玩法本身’（Gameplay）天然具备超越一切文化国界的普世力量。举个例子，就像‘投球与接球’这种朴素的人类身体游戏，全世界任何国家、任何语言的孩子都能在三秒内玩到一起。《宝可梦》的核心也是如此——在草丛中邂逅未知生命、心跳加速地收服它们、与同伴自豪地对战交换，这种植根于人性底层的游戏乐趣是相通的。
- speaker: Ohmori
  role: answer
  chapter: 全球共鸣与计步器
  original: "The Pokéwalker idea came up when we thought about what we could do to make players’ dream—'I would like to become a real Pokémon Trainer!'—possible. Our focus was on enabling a player to be with his or her Pokémon all the time, even when just walking around. Because you can play anytime, anywhere, it makes Pokémon a part of your daily life."
  translation: 【大森滋】：推出‘宝可计步器’（Pokéwalker）的灵感，源于我们一直在思考如何实现所有玩家自童年以来的终极梦想——‘好想成为一名真正生活在现实中的宝可梦训练家啊！’我们设计的唯一核心，就是让玩家即便只是在现实中通勤、散步，也能无时无刻与自己最珍爱的宝可梦相伴同行。随时随地，让宝可梦成为你生命呼吸的一部分。
