  original: Meet the Pokémon developers and learn what goes into making such incredible games!

- type: paragraph
  original: 'Recently, we had the rare opportunity to discuss Pokémon HeartGold and SoulSilver Versions with some very knowledgable people: the developers at GAME FREAK, inc. The six developers took time out to provide some insight into topics surrounding the games.'
  translation: 最近，我们难得有机会与 GAME FREAK 公司的开发者们——一群非常博学的人——讨论了《宝可梦 心金／魂银》。六位开发者抽出时间，就围绕游戏的话题提供了一些见解。
  note: 《宝可梦 心金／魂银》是《宝可梦 金／银》的重制版，于2009年发售。
- type: paragraph
  original: It has been ten years since the original Pokémon Gold and Silver versions. What has changed in that time with regards to your approach to game design and what has remained the same?
  translation: 距离最初的《宝可梦 金／银》已经过去了十年。在这段时间里，你们在游戏设计方法上有什么变化？又有哪些保持不变？
- type: paragraph
  original: Because of the hardware, our game design has changed dramatically. Really, I didn’t think of the Internet ten years ago! ( laughs ) Also, the quality of both picture and sound have dramatically advanced in that time—the number of colors and three-dimensional graphics, for instance, has greatly expanded. Of course, that made things harder! ( laughs ) One thing which hasn’t changed is our desire to create a game that is approachable for everyone.
  translation: 由于硬件的原因，我们的游戏设计发生了巨大变化。说实话，十年前我根本没想过互联网！（笑）同时，画面和声音的质量在那段时间里也大幅提升——例如，色彩数量和三维图形都大大扩展了。当然，这也让事情变得更难了！（笑）有一点没有改变，那就是我们希望创造一款人人都能轻松上手的游戏。
  speaker: 增田顺一
  note: 增田顺一（Junichi Masuda）是 GAME FREAK 的董事，也是宝可梦系列的音乐作曲家和游戏总监。
- type: paragraph
  original: It’s also worth noting that what has changed was that more people are now involved in the development. As a result, there are a lot of things we can do, but now we need somebody who can lead and hold the team together. Our passion for game creation has remained the same.
  translation: 同样值得注意的是，变化在于现在有更多人参与开发。因此，我们能做的事情很多，但现在我们需要有人能够领导并凝聚团队。我们对游戏创作的热情始终如一。
  speaker: 森本茂树
  note: 森本茂树（Shigeki Morimoto）是 GAME FREAK 的游戏总监，曾参与宝可梦系列多部作品的开发。
- type: paragraph
  original: Some things that have never changed are the basic, fun elements of the Pokémon games—collecting, battling, and trading—as well as the Pokémon themselves. But on the other hand, one primary thing that has changed is that new ideas come up due to the technical advances in the hardware.
  translation: 从未改变的是宝可梦游戏的基本乐趣元素——收集、对战和交换——以及宝可梦本身。但另一方面，一个主要的变化是，由于硬件技术的进步，新的想法不断涌现。
  speaker: 松岛贤二
  note: 松岛贤二（Kenji Matsushima）是 GAME FREAK 的策划，参与了宝可梦系列的游戏设计。
- type: paragraph
  original: When Gold and Silver launched, I was a student, and I played the game intensely. I never thought I would be involved in Pokémon game development. For me personally, this has been a huge change. I still have vivid memories of playing those games, and it encourages me to do my best to make an even better game for future players.
  translation: 当《金／银》发售时，我还是一名学生，并且狂热地游玩了这些游戏。我从未想过自己会参与宝可梦游戏的开发。对我个人而言，这是一个巨大的转变。我至今仍清晰地记得游玩那些游戏的记忆，这激励我尽最大努力为未来的玩家制作更好的游戏。
  speaker: 大森滋
- type: paragraph
  original: Most players will know that one thing that has changed is that the number of Pokémon has doubled— as a result, the data sizes have increased as well. This means we that need to make sure that one new idea is not going to affect any other Pokémon data. But one thing that has never changed is that we are a group of "Game Freaks," just like our company name says.
  translation: 大多数玩家都知道，变化之一是宝可梦的数量翻了一番——因此，数据量也随之增加。这意味着我们需要确保一个新想法不会影响到其他宝可梦的数据。但有一件事从未改变：我们是一群“游戏狂人”，正如我们公司名称所表明的那样。
  speaker: 森昭人
  note: Game Freak 是开发公司名称，字面意为“游戏狂人”，此处为双关。
- type: paragraph
  original: As we’ve noted, we are now able to express various aspects of the games differently thanks to the technological advancement of the hardware. However, our stance on precisely what new technology can be utilized hasn’t changed. We do not use all available technology—instead, we select those that are best for the players and those that make the gameplay more compelling.
  translation: 正如我们所说，得益于硬件的技术进步，我们现在能够以不同的方式表现游戏的各个方面。然而，关于究竟可以利用哪些新技术的立场并未改变。我们不会使用所有可用的技术，而是选择那些最适合玩家、能让游戏玩法更具吸引力的技术。
  speaker: 海野隆雄
- type: paragraph
  original: It's unusual for a game to be very popular both in Japan and in the United States (and for that matter, in Europe). What makes Pokémon so universally enjoyed?
  translation: 一款游戏能同时在日本和美国（乃至欧洲）都非常受欢迎，这并不常见。是什么让宝可梦如此受到普遍喜爱？
- type: paragraph
  original: 'First of all, it is not just a game series that simply presents the Pokémon world, characters, and scenarios. It is the entire gameplay experience that goes way beyond the borders. For instance, let’s take a universal play mechanic like throwing and catching a ball. Children from different countries can play that game right away. But now let’s consider another example: sports. Both of these share the same thing, and that’s gameplay. In Pokémon games, it is a game in which a player encounters Pokémon that have jumped out of the bushes, a game in which a player battles with a Pokémon and catches it, and a game in which a player can battle against other Trainers. Those are the basics. But then you have characters, scenarios, Pokédex, Items, moves, Trainers, and Rivals are intertwined with this basic gameplay. So, to answer your question, what makes Pokémon so universally enjoyed? It is because at the core of all our titles is gameplay.'
  translation: 首先，它不仅仅是一个简单呈现宝可梦世界、角色和剧情的游戏系列。它是超越国界的整体游戏体验。例如，让我们以投掷和接住球这样的普遍游戏机制为例。不同国家的孩子可以立即玩那个游戏。但现在让我们考虑另一个例子：体育运动。这两者都有共同点，那就是玩法。在宝可梦游戏中，玩家会遇到从草丛中跳出的宝可梦，与宝可梦战斗并捕捉它，还可以与其他训练家对战。这些是基础。但然后你还有角色、剧情、宝可梦图鉴、道具、招式、训练家和劲敌，它们与基础玩法交织在一起。所以，回答你的问题，是什么让宝可梦如此普遍受欢迎？那是因为我们所有作品的核心都是玩法。
  speaker: 增田顺一
- type: paragraph
  original: The Pokéwalker is not the first time Pokémon has made an accessory for the games . What do you like about the Pokéwalker concept that made you decide to add one to this game?
  translation: 宝可计步器并不是宝可梦第一次为游戏制作配件。你喜欢宝可计步器概念的哪些方面，以至于决定将其添加到这款游戏中？
  note: Pokéwalker 官方中文名为“宝可计步器”，是《心金／魂银》的配套外设。
- type: paragraph
  original: The idea came up when we thought about what we could do to make players’ dream—"I would like to become a real Pokémon Trainer!"—possible. Our focus was on enabling a player to be with his or her Pokémon all the time, even when just walking around. Because you can play anytime, anywhere, it seems like as many players play with their Pokéwalkers as play the actual games. I am happy to see that.
  translation: 这个想法源于我们思考如何让玩家“我想成为真正的宝可梦训练家！”的梦想成为可能。我们的重点是让玩家能够随时与自己的宝可梦在一起，即使只是走路时。因为你可以随时随地游玩，所以似乎使用宝可计步器的玩家和游玩游戏本身的玩家一样多。我很高兴看到这一点。
  speaker: 大森滋
- type: paragraph
  original: American gamers are becoming more interested in the development of the games they love. Can you give some insight into what it takes to make games like Pokémon HeartGold and SoulSilver Versions?
  translation: 美国玩家对他们所喜爱的游戏的开发越来越感兴趣。您能透露一些制作《宝可梦 心金／魂银》这样的游戏需要什么吗？
- type: paragraph
  original: Heart and Soul! ( laughs ) It took just under three years to develop and about twenty people were involved.
  translation: 心和魂！（笑）开发时间不到三年，大约有二十人参与。
  speaker: 森本茂树
  note: 森本茂树是 Game Freak 的导演，此处玩味游戏标题中的“心金／魂银”。
- type: paragraph
  original: Are there any particular areas or locations in Pokémon HeartGold and SoulSilver Versions that you are proud of or had a lot of fun developing? Any insider tips on what gamers definitely watch for or locations they absolutely must travel to?
  translation: 在《宝可梦 心金／魂银》中，有没有哪些特定区域或地点是你们引以为傲或开发时充满乐趣的？有没有什么内部提示，告诉玩家一定要留意什么或绝对要去哪些地点？
- type: paragraph
  original: My favorite place is Ecruteak City. It was the first town/city that we created for HeartGold and SoulSilver .
  translation: 我最喜欢的地方是缘朱市。那是我们为《心金／魂银》创造的第一个城镇。
  speaker: 森本茂树
- type: paragraph
  original: The Pokémon world is unique because of the liveliness of the Pokémon. And in HeartGold and SoulSilver , you can walk with Pokémon. I encourage players to try to walk with a wide variety of Pokémon. Travel around so you can meet the main characters, such as the Gym Leaders who appear in different parts of the regions. And there are various events in which you’ll earn their friendship.
  translation: 宝可梦世界之所以独特，是因为宝可梦的活力。在《心金／魂银》中，你可以和宝可梦一起行走。我鼓励玩家尝试与各种各样的宝可梦一起行走。四处旅行，这样你就能遇到主要角色，比如出现在各地区不同部分的道馆馆主。还有各种活动，你可以赢得他们的友谊。
  speaker: 松岛贤二
  note: 松岛（Matsushima）可能为松岛贤治（Kenji Matsushima），但此处未提供全名。
- type: paragraph
