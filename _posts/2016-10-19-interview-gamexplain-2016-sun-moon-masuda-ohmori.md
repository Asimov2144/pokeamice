- role: answer
  original: 'A: So in terms of making Pokemon games in 3D from a graphical perspective of course with X & Y and Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire we did our best at the time and felt like we maximized our output. We also went back and changed it to you’d now have full analog movement in the games and changed it so that human characters have more realistic proportions. - role: question
  chapter: 阿罗拉的样子的构思由来
  original: 'Q: What made you want to introduce Alola forms?'
  translation: 是什么让你们决定引入“阿罗拉的样子”？
- role: answer
  speaker: 大森滋
  original: 'A: So this being a 20th anniv. Title, I felt like this was a good moment to reflect back on Pokemon and what are Pokemon--these living creatures. And one of the ideas I got that might be interesting was that Pokemon that moved to the Alola region would evolve into unique ways kind of similar to what you see in the Galapagos islands so that’s where the inspiration came from. And we really went with the Pokemon that originally appeared in the Kanto region because they were the most recognized by long-term fans and we felt like we could express this concept of unique evolution best with these most recognized Pokemon so one example is the Eggsecutor, its neck grows really long in the Alola region. We worked on each of the original Kanto Pokemon and see how they would adapt in the Alola environment.'
  translation: 本作正值系列 20 周年，我觉得这是一个很好的契机，可以回头重新思考“宝可梦究竟是什么”——它们本身都是鲜活的生物。我当时想到的一个有趣点子是：如果原本栖息在别处的宝可梦迁徙到阿罗拉地区，或许会像加拉帕戈斯群岛的生物那样走出独特的演化道路，灵感正是源于此。我们特意挑选了最早登场于关都地区的宝可梦，因为老粉丝对它们的认知度最高，用这些最广为人知的宝可梦，最能把这种独特演化的概念表现出来。比如椰蛋树就是一个例子，它在阿罗拉地区脖子长得特别长。我们逐一审视了最初的关都地区宝可梦，推演它们会如何适应阿罗拉的环境。

- role: question
  chapter: 从像素到 3D 的宝可梦设计流程演变
  original: 'Q: Has the design process for creating new Pokemon changed over the years? Is it different for Sun & Moon?'
  translation: 这么多年来，设计新宝可梦的流程发生过变化吗？《太阳·月亮》和以往有所不同吗？
- role: answer
  speaker: 增田顺一
  original: 'A: Yeah there are definitely quite a few differences. In the very beginning for example, we would just be designing the Pokemon as pixel art right from the get go. And then that’s how we did it for a while and then starting with Diamond and Pearl we would move over the process of actually doing the illustrations and designs separately and then from there translate them from there back into pixel art for the game. Now with 3D though, even at that original stage of coming up with design illustrations, we have to think about how they move for example in 3D and then we have to make their 3D models. So there’s a lot more considerations.'
  translation: 是的，确实有不少变化。比如在最早期，我们一开始就是直接以像素画的形式来设计宝可梦的。这种做法持续了一段时间，直到从《宝可梦 钻石·珍珠》开始，我们才转为先单独绘制设定插画与造型设计，之后再将其转化为游戏内使用的像素点阵图。而如今进入 3D 时代，即便还在最初构思设定图稿的阶段，我们也必须一并考虑它们在三维空间里如何活动，随后再制作对应的 3D 模型。因此需要兼顾的环节比过去多了很多。
  note: 发言者据配套视频摘要（“according to Mr. Masuda”）及下文转由大森滋补充的标注核定为增田顺一。
- role: answer
  speaker: 大森滋
  original: 'A: (From Mr Ohmori) More speaking about the design process in general, we really do get ideas and requests from a variety of different team members. For example, maybe someone might want to use a specific animal as inspiration for Pokemon--they have a great idea to do that. Or our battle system planners--they need a Pokemon to fill this role in battle or achieve a certain balance or also for story reasons, people want to fit a Pokémon into a role in the story so lots of requests lots of ideas come up and we have to work really closely with the designers and artists and find a right mix a right balance of Pokemon. It’s really because we have so many people coming up with requests and ideas that we have such a wide variety of Pokemon in the games.'
  translation: 从更整体的设计流程来说，团队里各个岗位的成员都会提出五花八门的点子和需求。比方说，也许有人想以某种特定动物为原型来设计宝可梦，恰好冒出了一个很棒的构想；又或者负责对战系统的企划，需要一只宝可梦在战斗中承担特定定位、维持某方面的平衡；也有出于剧情需要，希望让某只宝可梦在故事里扮演特定角色。像这样各种需求和创意不断涌现，我们就需要与设计师、美术人员紧密协作，从中拿捏出恰当的组合与整体平衡。正是因为有这么多成员共同提出诉求与点子，游戏里的宝可梦种类才会如此丰富多彩。

- role: question
  chapter: 跨越作品的统一宝可梦世界观
  original: 'Q: In Sun & Moon, are you focused on expanding the shared lore across the games?'

- role: answer
  speaker: 大森滋
  original: 'A: So yeah the way I would answer that I think it’s a unique trait of Pokemon that there’s really no old and new. We always want to expand on this Pokemon universe that’s why we have some characters from previous games appearing in the current games. Also with Pokemon we kind of treat them all as if they’re all current, like CHarizard is still just as popular as it was before and can still be just as active.'
  translation: 对于这个问题，我觉得《宝可梦》有一个很独特的特质，就是这里其实没有所谓的“新”与“旧”。我们始终努力让所有要素都处于同一平面上并存。这个世界里有许许多多不同的地区，尽管分别出自不同作品，但它们都在同一片天地中共存。我们一直希望不断拓展这个宝可梦世界，这也是前作角色会在新作中登场的原因。对待宝可梦本身也是如此，我们会把它们全都视作当下的存在——好比喷火龙，如今依然和从前一样受欢迎，也同样能够活跃在舞台上。
