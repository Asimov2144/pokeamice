- type: heading

  original: "Black and White: polarity"
  translation: 黑与白：两极性
- speaker: Nintendo Power
  role: question
  original: "After years of progressively more exotic titles, is there any special significance to returning to basic colors with black and white?"
  translation: 多年来游戏标题逐渐变得更具奇幻色彩，如今回到黑与白这两种基础颜色，有什么特别含义吗？
- speaker: Junichi Masuda
  role: answer
  original: >-
    “We choose the titles to express the themes of each game. Like, diamonds are the hardest substance in the world, so that fit the theme of ‘ultimateness’ in Pokemon Diamond and Pearl. This time we’re pursuing a theme of ‘polarity,’ things that are defined by their opposition to each other. Take, for example, angels and demons, or light and shadow—if one didn’t exist, the other would lose all meaning. ‘White’ is a blank slate with potential to become anything, while ‘black’ is complete and unchanging. So our intention actually wasn’t to return to the basics, but to evoke that particular theme.”
  translation: >-
    “我们选择标题，是为了表达每款游戏的主题。比如，钻石是世界上最坚硬的物质，因而呼应了《钻石／珍珠》的‘终极’主题。这次我们追求的是‘两极性’——彼此对立、相互定义的事物。天使与恶魔、光与影都是这样的例子：如果一方不存在，另一方也会失去意义。‘白’像一张空白画布，拥有变成任何事物的潜力；‘黑’则是完整而不变的。因此，我们并非想回归基础，而是想呈现这个特定主题。”
- type: heading
  level: 2
  original: "Designing and selecting Pokémon"
  translation: 设计流程与入选标准
- speaker: Nintendo Power
  role: question
  original: "What is the process for designing new Pokemon? How do you decide which ones make the cut?"
  translation: 设计新宝可梦的流程是怎样的？你们如何决定哪些设计能够入选？
- speaker: Ken Sugimori
  role: answer
  original: >-
    “We probably design around three times more Pokemon than we intend to actually use in the game. The way it works is, each designer brings in rough sketches of their designs. I meet with them individually to discuss certain features of their designs, and they bring them back with the revisions. If the Pokemon makes the cut, I will draw its official art.”
  translation: >-
    “我们设计的宝可梦数量大约是游戏最终采用数量的三倍。流程是每位设计师先提交草图，我会分别与他们讨论设计中的一些特点，再由他们修改。如果某只宝可梦最终入选，我就会为它绘制正式设定图。”
- speaker: Dr. Lava
  role: answer
  original: >-
    Dr Lava’s note, paraphrased: Sugimori says the team designs roughly three times the number ultimately used. Applied to Unova’s 156 new Pokémon, that suggests about 468 designs, or broadly 400–500. This is the author’s extrapolation, not a total Sugimori states in the interview.
  translation: >-
    Dr. Lava 注（作者推算）：杉森说团队设计量约为最终采用量的三倍。以合众新增的156只计算，约为468份设计，因此作者概括为400至500份。这个范围是作者按比例推算出来的，不是杉森在访谈中报出的确数。
- type: heading
  level: 2
  original: "Keeping favorite Pokémon across generations"
  translation: 跨世代带着自己喜欢的宝可梦
- speaker: Nintendo Power
  role: question
  original: "And you don’t ever think, “let’s make our lives easier by cutting the least popular Pokemon out of the franchise”?"
  translation: 你们有没有想过：“把最不受欢迎的宝可梦从系列中删掉，让工作轻松些”？
- speaker: Junichi Masuda
  role: answer
  original: >-
    “[Laughs] I think one of the joys of Pokemon is being able to use your favorite Pokemon from the Blue and Red Versions all the way through to the newest iterations. So no, I don’t think we can do that.”
  translation: >-
    “（笑）我觉得宝可梦的一大乐趣，就是从《红／蓝》到最新作品，玩家都能使用自己最喜欢的宝可梦。所以，不，我认为我们不能那么做。”
- speaker: Dr. Lava
  role: answer
  original: >-
    Dr Lava’s retrospective commentary, paraphrased: After the 2019 Sword & Shield roster announcement, some fans objected that not every Pokémon would carry forward. He connects that reaction to Masuda’s earlier answer and says the contrast is unfortunate. This is his later opinion, not part of the 2011 interview.
  translation: >-
    Dr. Lava 注（事后评论）：2019年《剑／盾》公布并非所有旧宝可梦都会登场后，一些玩家对此表示不满。作者把这场争议与增田当年的回答联系起来，并认为两者之间的落差令人遗憾。这是 Dr. Lava 后来的评论，不是2011年的访谈内容。
