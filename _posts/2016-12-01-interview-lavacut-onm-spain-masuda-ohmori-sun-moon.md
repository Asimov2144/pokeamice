- original: 'The following interview was originally published in late 2016, in the Spanish edition of Official Nintendo Magazine. The interview was conducted shortly after the worldwide release of Pokemon Sun & Moon, and featured Sun & Moon’s director Shigeru Ohmori, and the games’ producer Junichi Masuda. It’s a pretty short interview but includes some interesting quotes, including brief references to Pokemon Gray, Pokemon Z, and the prospect of new Pokemon Colosseum. A big thanks to Feroz El Mejor for submitting magazine photos, so this interview can be preserved and translated into English. Here’s the interview:'
  translation: "以下访谈最初发表于2016年底的西班牙版《官方任天堂杂志》。访谈在《宝可梦 太阳·月亮》全球发售不久后进行，受访者为该作总监大森滋与制作人增田顺一。访谈篇幅不长，但包含一些有趣的发言，其中简要提到了《宝可梦 灰》《宝可梦 Z》以及新的《宝可梦圆形竞技场》的可能性。非常感谢 Feroz El Mejor 提供杂志照片，使这篇访谈得以保存并译成英文。以下是访谈内容："

  role: question
  speaker: Official Nintendo Magazine
  translation: "“《宝可梦 太阳·月亮》在许多国家打破了销售纪录，包括西班牙。你们预料到会获得这样的反响吗？”"
- original: “I put a lot of effort into making them, and for me it’s an honor and a great joy they’re being enjoyed so much in Spain and all over the world.”
  role: answer
  speaker: 大森滋
  translation: “我在制作上投入了很多精力，看到它们在西班牙和全世界如此受欢迎，对我来说是一种荣誉，也是极大的喜悦。”
- original: “This year Pokemon regained much of its popularity (which wasn’t easy) thanks to Pokemon GO. Do you think GO’s success contributed to the great triumph of Pokemon Sun & Moon?”
  role: question
  speaker: Official Nintendo Magazine
  translation: "“今年多亏了《Pokémon GO》，宝可梦重新获得了很高的人气（这并不容易）。你们认为《Pokémon GO》的成功对《宝可梦 太阳·月亮》的大获成功有贡献吗？”"
- original: “This year marks the 20th anniversary of the Pokemon series, and it coincided with the launch of Pokemon GO; I think these two factors together helped a lot in making Sun & Moon so successful.”
  role: answer
  speaker: 大森滋
  translation: "“今年是宝可梦系列20周年，又恰逢《Pokémon GO》上线；我认为这两个因素共同促成了《太阳·月亮》的成功。”"
- original: “We really like the three new starter Pokemon in the seventh generation. Which ones are your favorites and why?”
  role: question
  speaker: Official Nintendo Magazine
  translation: “我们非常喜欢第七代的三只新初始宝可梦。你们最喜欢哪只，为什么？”

  role: answer
  speaker: 增田顺一
  translation: “我家里养了猫，所以我最喜欢火斑喵。现在我的火斑喵已经进化了，但我还记得它之前的样子，那让我想起美好的回忆。”
- original: “The one I like the most is Rowlett because with the jump to three dimensions, we can see how his head turns, “chirp chirp chirp,” which makes him the most fun and interesting.”
  role: answer
  speaker: 大森滋
  translation: “我最喜欢的是木木枭，因为随着画面进入三维，我们可以看到它的头转动，‘啾啾啾’，这使它成为最有趣、最引人注目的一只。”
- original: “Pokemon Sun & Moon is a huge and very complete game, but were there any features you would have liked to include in Sun & Moon, but due to lack of time or other reasons weren’t possible?”
  role: question
  speaker: Official Nintendo Magazine
  translation: "“《宝可梦 太阳·月亮》是一款庞大且非常完整的游戏，但有没有哪些内容你们本想加入《太阳·月亮》，却因为时间不足或其他原因而未能实现？”"
- original: “There were lots of staff working on development, and we all contribute many ideas. The process is always to choose the ideas that are the best or the most feasible, and discard the ones that aren’t. So ultimately, there’s always lots of stuff that doesn’t get implemented.”
  role: answer
  speaker: 大森滋
  translation: “参与开发的工作人员很多，大家都会提出许多想法。流程始终是挑选出最好或最可行的想法，舍弃不行的。所以最终总会有很多内容没能实现。”
- original: “What are your thoughts on the great achievement of the player who managed to defeat the Elite 4 using nothing but a Magikarp? Did you think something like that was possible?”
  role: question
  speaker: Official Nintendo Magazine
  translation: “有位玩家仅用一只鲤鱼王就击败了四天王，对于这一壮举您怎么看？您觉得这种事是可能的吗？”
- original: “There’s always someone who’s capable of doing things we didn’t think were possible. Hearing about those kinds of accomplishments really amuses us. Fans are always surprising us in new ways.”
  role: answer
  speaker: 增田顺一
  translation: “总有人能做到我们以为不可能的事。听到这类成就，我们真的觉得很有趣。粉丝总是以新的方式让我们惊讶。”

  speaker: Official Nintendo Magazine

  role: answer
  speaker: 增田顺一

  role: question

  role: answer

  role: question
  speaker: Official Nintendo Magazine
  translation: “为什么我们最终没能见到《宝可梦Z》？”
- original: “We never really planned to make a third version, we always want to do things that surprise fans. For example, after Black & White people thought Pokemon Gray was going to come next, but instead we made Black & White 2. And the same with X & Y, instead we made Omega Ruby & Alpha Sapphire. People thought Pokemon Z would come next, but taking advantage of the fact it’s the 20th anniversary, we decided to deliver another surprise with Sun & Moon.”
  role: answer
  speaker: 增田顺一
  translation: "“我们其实从未计划做第三版本，我们一直想做能让粉丝惊喜的东西。比如《黑·白》之后，大家以为接下来会是《宝可梦灰》，但我们做的是《黑2·白2》。《X·Y》也一样，我们做的是《欧米伽红宝石·阿尔法蓝宝石》。大家以为接下来会是《宝可梦Z》，但我们借着20周年的机会，决定用《太阳·月亮》再带来一次惊喜。”"

  role: answer
  speaker: 增田顺一
