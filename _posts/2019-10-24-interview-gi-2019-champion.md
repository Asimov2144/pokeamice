- original: What was the idea behind having regular interactions with the League Champion, Leon, from the earliest stages in the game?
  role: question
  speaker: Game Informer
  translation: 让玩家从游戏最早期阶段就能与联盟冠军丹帝进行日常互动，这个想法背后是什么？
- original: One of the biggest reasons for having you interact with the champion and being shown the prowess of the champion so early is that Sword and Shield is a continuation of traditional RPGs like X and Y, Sun and Moon. So really conveying that aspect of you’re going to go on your journey, doing the gym battles, and there’s this really powerful champion you want to aspire to defeat. That was one of the aspects we wanted to convey, so we put the champion in the story much more.
  role: answer
  speaker: 大森滋
  translation: 让你与冠军互动、并在这么早的阶段就展示冠军的实力，其中一个最大的原因是，《剑／盾》是《X／Y》《太阳／月亮》这类传统 RPG 的延续。所以，真正传达出这一点很重要：你将踏上旅程，去打道馆战，而有一位非常强大的冠军是你想要去击败的。这是我想传达的方面之一，所以我们在故事里加入了更多冠军的戏份。

- original: When you’re in the Wild Area, do you have to set your game to a certain status so players can interact with you?
  role: question
  speaker: Game Informer
  translation: 当你在旷野地带时，是否必须把游戏设定成某种状态，其他玩家才能与你互动？
- original: So how it works is there’s this feature called the Y-Comm, and you can activate that to broadcast your status or what you want to do, like, “I want to trade,” “I want to battle,” or whatever, and then that gets broadcasted out to anyone you’ve been matched up with in the back end. They’ll see that, and if they want to reply or if they’re also saying they want to battle, it’ll match you together and then it goes into a synchronous connection.
  role: answer
  speaker: 大森滋
  translation: 它的运作方式是这样的：有一个叫 Y-Comm 的功能，你可以激活它来广播你的状态或你想做的事，比如“我想交换”“我想对战”之类的，然后这会广播给后台匹配到的任何人。他们会看到，如果他们想回应，或者他们也在说想对战，就会把你们匹配到一起，然后进入同步连接。
- original: One of the cool things you can do now is – in previous games, we had Wonder Trade where you could throw out a Pokémon and it would find someone to trade with and the trade would happen and it would save the game. It was a very active process. We’ve kind of improved that. So now, [with Surprise Trades], you can choose a Pokémon for trading, and then it’ll happen in the background, so while you’re just adventuring, it’ll find someone to trade with and after some times progresses, you’ll suddenly see that your Pokémon was traded for something else. We found a way to make it happen more seamlessly without having to save and actively do it one at a time.
  role: answer
  speaker: 大森滋
  translation: 现在你能做的一件很酷的事是——在以前的游戏里，我们有奇迹交换，你可以扔出一只宝可梦，它会找到人交换，交换完成后会保存游戏。那是一个非常主动的过程。我们算是改进了这一点。所以现在，[通过惊喜交换]，你可以选一只宝可梦用于交换，然后它会在后台进行，你只管冒险，它会找到人交换，过一段时间后，你会突然发现你的宝可梦被换成了别的东西。我们找到了一种方式，让它更无缝地进行，不必保存、也不必一次一只地主动操作。
- original: And it’s also not just exclusive to the Wild Area. I know we’re just talking about the Wild Area right now, but this is probably going to be one of the more popular features that players engage with in the game.
  role: answer
  speaker: 增田顺一
  translation: 而且这也不仅限于旷野地带。我知道我们现在只是在聊旷野地带，但这很可能会成为玩家在游戏里使用的更受欢迎的功能之一。

- original: What’s the deal with the Pokémon timeline?
  role: question
  speaker: Game Informer
  translation: 宝可梦的时间线到底是怎么回事？
- original: It starts to get a little complicated if you pay too much attention to timelines. Like, there might be a professor that appears and it wouldn’t make sense at all if we applied that kind of timeline logic. So we try not to apply it too rigorously. Maybe one hint is that if a character is appearing with Professor Oak, they’re living in the same era. Rather than some series where it makes sense to have the timeline progress as you go and the story evolve, the approach that Pokémon takes is expanding the world, like what the regions are, and making it richer as we go. Rather than a timeline, it’s more of a physical space thing.
  role: answer
  speaker: 增田顺一
  translation: 如果太在意时间线，事情就会变得有点复杂。比如，可能会出现某位博士，要是套用那种时间线逻辑，就完全说不通了。所以我们尽量不把它套得太严格。也许有一个线索是，如果某个角色和大木博士一起出现，那他们就生活在同一个时代。不像某些系列，时间线随作品推进、故事随之演变才说得通，宝可梦采取的做法是扩展世界，比如各个地区是什么样，并随着作品不断让它更丰富。与其说是时间线，不如说是物理空间上的东西。

  role: question
  speaker: Game Informer

  role: answer

  role: question
  speaker: Game Informer
  translation: 所以这似乎是在流程中相当靠后的阶段？

  role: answer

[Laughs]

  role: answer
