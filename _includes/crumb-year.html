{%- comment -%} What a year crumb needs: how many posts the year has, and whether the timeline has a page for it. Takes `year` (a string). Prints "N|1" or "N|0"; the caller captures and splits it. Meant for include_cached, so the pass over site.posts runs once per year rather than once per page. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign cy_n = 0 -%} {%- for cy_post in site.posts -%} {%- assign cy_y = cy_post.date | date: "%Y" -%} {%- if cy_y == include.year -%}{%- assign cy_n = cy_n | plus: 1 -%}{%- endif -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- assign cy_url = "/timeline/" | append: include.year | append: "/" -%} {%- assign cy_page = site.pages | where: "url", cy_url | first -%} {{ cy_n }}|{% if cy_page %}1{% else %}0{% endif %}
