A person: the first sentence of the profile summary (tools/build-people-profiles.py, written from the person's own words in the library, [n] marks removed) - unless that sentence only says what the material lacks - followed by the count and span of their credits and how many entries the library holds on them. A staff-only person has no summary: the credits line (rule-written from the staff rolls, departments kept, leadership history dropped) and the latest game and role carry it.
