{%- comment -%} The filter bar above the cards (desktop; a phone keeps the chip rows of home-dimensions.html): one row of buttons - type, year, person, work - and each opens a panel as wide as the cards, so a year is a bar you can read, a person has a portrait and a name that fits, a work has its icon. It replaces the sidebar's dimension panel, which said the same things in 300px, and the four selects of the left rail (kept in the markup - they hold the filter's state - but hidden here). Every button carries data-dim / data-value and sets the catalogue's matching filter (assets/js/catalogue.js), which also writes the chosen value back onto the row's button ([data-facet-val]) and lets the reader look up a person or work that is not listed ([data-find]). Needs the counts of home-dimensions.html. {%- endcomment -%}
