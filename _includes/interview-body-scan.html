{%- comment -%} The body of a scanned magazine archive - CONTINUE, Nintendo DREAM - in two views that were one page before, and served neither reader well. READ turning the magazine: one page at a time, the region crops as figures with their captions, the body regions as prose, the notes as small asides. Nothing about how it was made. ARCHIVE the OCR record: every region on every page with its id, type, box, writing direction, review status, and the correction the proofreader logged. For whoever is checking the transcription. The data is translation_segments, one per OCR region, carrying scan_page, scan_box, region_type, review_status and comment; `speaker` on these is the region type (body, note, image, caption), which is why the old layout printed "body 受访" over every paragraph. It is never shown here. The full-page scans live under archive/, which the build excludes; only the region crops are in assets.
