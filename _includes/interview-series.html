{%- comment -%} Which chapters this interview belongs with, decided from the titles. The series posts - 社長が訊く, N.O.M, the recruit rounds - carry no series field; what they share is a title up to "第N章". So the key is the title before the first 第 whose next characters are a chapter number, and the series is every interview whose title contains that key. Sorted by title, which orders single-digit chapters correctly; none of the series runs past 9. A serialised column (first N回, the Shudo memoir) is the same shape but runs to three digits, so that kind is sorted by date instead. Sets series_key, series_label, series_unit (章 or 回), series_posts, series_index (1-based), series_prev, series_next. Empty when the post is not part of a series. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign series_key = "" -%} {%- assign series_label = "" -%} {%- assign series_posts = "" | split: "" -%} {%- assign series_prev = nil -%} {%- assign series_next = nil -%} {%- assign series_index = 0 -%} {%- comment -%} 第N章 for the interview series; 第N回 for a serialised column such as the Shudo memoir {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign series_by = "title" -%} {%- assign series_unit = "章" -%} {%- if page.title contains "第" -%} {%- assign series_head = page.title | split: "第" | first | strip -%} {%- assign series_tail = page.title | remove_first: series_head | strip | remove_first: "第" | slice: 0, 5 -%} {%- assign series_head_len = series_head | size -%} {%- if series_tail contains "回" -%}{%- assign series_by = "date" -%}{%- assign series_unit = "回" -%}{%- endif -%} {%- if series_head_len >= 6 and series_tail contains "章" or series_head_len >= 6 and series_tail contains "回" -%} {%- assign series_key = series_head -%} {%- comment -%} the key without the archive's own prefix, for stripping off member titles that have lost theirs {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign series_label = series_head | remove: "[访谈翻译] " | remove: "[扫描访谈] " | strip -%} {%- endif -%} {%- endif -%} {%- if series_key != "" -%} {%- assign series_found = "" | split: "" -%} {%- assign series_pool = site.posts | where_exp: "p", "p.layout == 'interview-editorial' or p.layout == 'parallel-translation'" -%} {%- for candidate in series_pool -%} {%- if candidate.title contains series_key -%} {%- assign series_found = series_found | push: candidate -%} {%- endif -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- if series_found.size > 1 -%} {%- assign series_posts = series_found | sort: series_by -%} {%- for member in series_posts -%} {%- if member.url == page.url -%} {%- assign series_index = forloop.index -%} {%- unless forloop.first -%}{%- assign prev_i = forloop.index0 | minus: 1 -%}{%- assign series_prev = series_posts[prev_i] -%}{%- endunless -%} {%- unless forloop.last -%}{%- assign series_next = series_posts[forloop.index] -%}{%- endunless -%} {%- endif -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- endif -%} {%- endif -%}
