Posted By GlitterBerri at 1:15 PM on Wednesday May 29, 2013

Translator’s Notes

And that brings our retrospective to a close. These might not be the only images related to Pokémon in the book they’re extracted from. If anyone has a copy of Satoshi Tajiri: The Man who Created Pokémon (ISBN: 4840127751), perhaps they might be willing to share more pictures and text, now that they can be deciphered.

This image, unfortunately, remains unreadable because of its low resolution. Anyone with further information or resources regarding early Pokémon is encouraged to get in touch with me at glitterberri.com@gmail.com, or Nat at Fuck Yeah, Beta Pokémon Art.

    The term “illusory monsters” is interesting because it is an actual term in the Pokémon series. In Japan, “illusory Pokémon” is a term, distinct from “legendary Pokémon”, that refers to the Pokémon that you can only get from an event. (Mew, Celebi, etc.) This was just translated as “Legendary” until recently. For the past few years, you’ll notice that Pokémon no longer officially calls these Pokémon “Legendary”. Now they use the term “Mythical”, which is equivalent to the Japanese “illusory Pokémon”.
