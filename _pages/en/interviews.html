How to read the interview archive

The archive has {% include inv.html key="kind_interview" lang="en" %} interview records. Use this selection to find a starting point, then follow a developer profile or game to related material. Interview records preserve the outlet, source language and translation status. Dates refer to the source, not the date the translation was added.

Source links can lead to a publisher or an archived copy. Historical URLs may no longer load; check the individual record for scans, snapshots and source notes. Japanese translations are AI generated and have not been human proofread.
