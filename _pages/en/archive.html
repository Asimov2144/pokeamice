Source recovery and reconstruction

Historical research needs more than an article title. A recruitment URL can change speakers, a magazine scan can lack pages, and a publisher's website can disappear. Source records connect translations to the version or scan used, so readers can inspect that context before citing a claim.

The archive includes {% include inv.html key="kind_scan" lang="en" %} scan records. Scans, OCR and translations have different error risks; individual pages record their sources and available workflow status. An archived snapshot date is not necessarily a publication date.
