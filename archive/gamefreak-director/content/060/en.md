After a long period of waiting,

Today, Thursday, September 28th, 2006.

We finally released

“Pokemon Diamond,” and

“Pokemon Pearl”!!

This morning, I went to see an opening event at

Takashimaya (a department store) in Shinjuku that

started from 7:45 am.

There were people who began standing in line last

night and spent the whole night there, and

others began standing in line from 4 am.

Such a lot of people were standing in line…

I was really moved.

It’s such a gratifying experience to actually

see the faces of those who play our games!

Anyway!

Please take the time to play the game thoroughly,

from beginning to end.

I’m sure you can find lots of new ways of playing it.

Don’t forget to try WiFi too.

Now!

Let’s start playing!
